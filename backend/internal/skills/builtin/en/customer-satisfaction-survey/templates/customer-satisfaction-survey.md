## Questions
1. How satisfied are you with [the purchase / the support / the solution] overall?
   Scale: 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied)
2. How easy was it to [get the solution / complete the process]?
   Scale: 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy)
3. How clear was the information you received?
   Scale: 1 (very unclear) to 5 (very clear)
4. (Optional) What influenced your rating the most?
   [Open answer]
