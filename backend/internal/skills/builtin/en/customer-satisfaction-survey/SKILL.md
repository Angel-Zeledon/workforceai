# Customer satisfaction survey

## When to use it

To measure customer satisfaction after a specific moment, such as a purchase, a delivery, a closed case or a training session. Use `templates/customer-satisfaction-survey.md` as the structure.

## Steps

1. Define the send moment and the interaction being rated, so the customer knows what the questions refer to.
2. Write an overall satisfaction question with a five- or seven-point scale, labels at both ends and wording that does not lean the answer.
3. Add one or two questions about aspects the team can change, such as speed, clarity or resolution, each with a scale of the same type.
4. Include an optional open question asking for the reason behind the rating, without asking for personal data.
5. Keep the survey to five questions at most, answerable in under two minutes.
6. Define how the answers will be used: who reviews them, how often, and which answer triggers a follow-up.

## Quality criteria

- No question contains two ideas at once.
- Questions do not suggest an answer or use sales language.
- Each question has an associated action; if it does not, it is not included.
- The survey can be answered in two minutes or less.

## Limits

Leave the survey as a draft for approval by the customer experience owner. It does not send surveys or contact customers. It does not offer incentives or discounts in exchange for responses and does not ask for unnecessary personal data.
