# Internal policy draft
## When to use it
To formalize a rule that affects all staff or a department, such as remote work, leave, travel or expenses, before presenting it to leadership.

## Steps
1. Define the topic, the owning department and the problem the policy solves, in one sentence.
2. Write the purpose and scope: who it applies to, which offices or countries, and from which date.
3. Write the rules with clear verbs (must, may, must not). Include figures and deadlines only if they appear in the available data.
4. Add the exceptions, how to request them and who approves them.
5. List owners, the contact channel, effective date, review date and related internal documents.
6. Fill in the template `templates/policy.md` and mark any missing data as pending.

## Quality criteria
- Each rule is verifiable and has an owner who applies it.
- Exceptions have clear criteria and a person who approves them.
- Neutral, inclusive language with no vague wording such as "as appropriate".
- Check the labor rules that apply in your country before setting rights, working hours or legal deadlines.

## Limits
This is a draft for review by HR and legal counsel; do not publish it or apply it to specific people. Do not invent amounts, days or rights that are not in the information.
