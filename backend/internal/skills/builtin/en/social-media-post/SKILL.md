# Social media post

## When to use it
To prepare brand posts for social networks with a specific goal.

## Steps
1. Define the platform, the audience and the goal: reach, engagement or clicks.
2. Write a first line with a hook that makes sense without context.
3. Develop a single core message in two or three sentences.
4. Add a clear call to action and, if relevant, the link provided in the task.
5. Suggest two to four relevant hashtags and respect the platform's length limit.

## Quality criteria
- Tone and length fit the chosen network.
- No more than one message or promise per post.
- Spelling, mentions and facts are checked before proposing it.

## Limits
Deliver the post as a draft for brand approval before publishing. Do not include figures, testimonials or mentions of people who are not in the data.
