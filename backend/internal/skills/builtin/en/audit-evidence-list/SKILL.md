# Audit evidence list

## When to use it
To prepare the documentation that an internal or external audit will request.

## Steps
1. Start from the list of controls or the areas the audit will review.
2. For each control, list the required evidence: document, record, approval or extract.
3. Assign the source, the responsible person and the period each evidence must cover.
4. Mark each item as available, pending or not applicable, with the reason.

## Quality criteria
- Each control has at least one linked evidence or a note explaining its absence.
- Dates and periods cover exactly the scope of the audit.
- No evidence is marked as available unless it was located in the data.

## Limits
The list organizes preparation and does not certify compliance with any control. Do not assume a document exists or invent references or dates.
