# Event plan

## When to use it
When organizing an internal or external event, to define what it should achieve and put the work in order before booking or contracting.

## Steps
1. Define the measurable goal of the event, the audience, the expected number of attendees and the tentative date.
2. Choose the venue and check capacity, accessibility, availability and what the rental includes.
3. Design the agenda with the time, duration and owner of each block, including arrival and closing.
4. List the suppliers or services needed (catering, audio, furniture, permits) and get at least two options per category to compare.
5. Build the budget by category with a contingency percentage, and compare it with the approved amount.
6. Build the timeline backwards from the event date, with deadlines for bookings, payments and confirmations. Fill in the template `templates/event-plan.md`.

## Quality criteria
- The goal is measurable and appears at the top of the plan.
- The agenda times add up and every block has an owner.
- The budget includes an explicit contingency and the total matches its categories.
- Every supplier has a confirmation deadline and an internal owner.

## Limits
This is a work plan for approval by the event owner. Do not confirm bookings, contracts or payments, and do not contact suppliers without human approval. Do not invent quotes, venue capacities or permits; if the event needs municipal permits, insurance or safety inspections, leave them as items to verify.
