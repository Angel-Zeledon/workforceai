# Funding call proposal
## When to use it
When responding to a public or private funding or grant call with defined requirements and a deadline.

## Steps
1. Read the call guidelines and record the requirements, eligibility, maximum amount, dates and required format.
2. Describe the need with verifiable data and explain how it relates to the objectives of the call.
3. Define two to four measurable objectives and, for each, the main activities with a timeline.
4. Present the budget by category, with the amounts requested and the own or third-party contributions.
5. Propose process and outcome indicators, with baseline, target and means of verification.
6. Fill in the template `templates/grant-proposal.md` and verify compliance with each requirement before submitting.

## Quality criteria
- Each requirement of the call has a visible answer in the proposal.
- Objectives, activities and budget are consistent with one another.
- Each indicator has a baseline, a target and a way to verify it.
- The proposal respects the length and format required by the guidelines.

## Limits
This is a draft for the person responsible at the organization; it does not submit applications or sign commitments with funders or entities. Do not invent need data, prior results, partnerships or co-funding, and do not promise targets without a basis.
