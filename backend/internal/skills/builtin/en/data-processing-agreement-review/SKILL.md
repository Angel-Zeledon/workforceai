# Data processing agreement review

## When to use it
When a vendor will process personal data on the company's behalf, or the other way around, and the processing agreement must be reviewed before it is signed.

## Steps
1. Identify the parties, who is the controller and who is the processor, and which types of data and data subjects are involved.
2. Verify that the purpose of processing is limited to what is stated and that the controller's instructions are in writing.
3. Review sub-processors: the list, prior notice, the right to object and responsibility for them.
4. Review security measures, the deadline and content of incident notifications, and support for answering data subject requests.
5. Review international transfers, where the data is located and the safeguards declared.
6. Review return or deletion at the end of the contract, with its deadline and proof. Check the data protection rules applicable in your country.
7. Deliver a findings table with clause, quoted text, risk and suggested change, and close with a recommendation.

## Quality criteria
- Each finding cites the exact clause and, if there is one, the annex.
- Missing obligations are flagged even if they are not written at all.
- Notification and deletion deadlines appear with their figure; if they do not exist, they are marked as absent.
- The output uses categories of data and data subjects, without real personal data.

## Limits
It is not legal advice and does not certify compliance with any rule or replace an impact assessment. A person competent in privacy or law must validate the agreement.
