# Lead qualification

## When to use it
When reviewing new prospects or a contact list to prioritize sales work.

## Steps
1. Define the fit criteria for the ideal customer: sector, size and location.
2. Score each criterion with the available evidence, marking it as confirmed or assumed.
3. Assess need, budget, decision authority and urgency.
4. Classify the lead as hot, warm or cold with a short justification.
5. Propose the next action for each lead according to its class.

## Quality criteria
- Each score is backed by a data point or marked as missing information.
- The classification applies the same criteria to every lead.
- The summary table fits on one screen.

## Limits
The qualification is a recommendation for the sales team, which decides whom to contact. Never assign a budget, title or interest that does not appear in the data.
