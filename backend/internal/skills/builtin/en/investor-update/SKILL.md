# Investor update
## When to use it
At the close of each reporting period agreed with investors, to report progress, figures and the support the company needs.

## Steps
1. Open with three to five achievements of the period, each with its measurable effect on the business.
2. Present the agreed metrics with the period value, the prior-period value and the target, always using the same definition.
3. Report the cash position: closing balance, average monthly burn and months of runway, with a cut-off date.
4. Explain the main challenges with the action under way and the deadline to resolve them.
5. Close with specific asks: introductions, presentations, decisions or support, with who and by when.
6. Fill in the template `templates/investor-update.md` and request review from finance and management before sending.

## Quality criteria
- Each metric has a definition, a cut-off date and a comparison with the prior period.
- Cash is presented with balance, burn and runway calculated with the same formula each period.
- Challenges include what is being done and when, not only the problem.
- The asks are specific, with an owner and a deadline.

## Limits
This is a draft: it is not sent to investors without human approval from management and finance. Do not invent metrics, projections or cash figures; if a figure is missing, mark it "pending". Do not include confidential customer or third-party information that is not authorized to be shared.
