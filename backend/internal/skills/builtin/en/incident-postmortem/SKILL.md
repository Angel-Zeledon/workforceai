# Incident postmortem
## When to use it
After resolving a significant incident, to learn from it without assigning blame.

## Steps
1. Summarize the incident in three or four sentences: what happened, when, how long it lasted and which service was affected.
2. Build a timeline with time and event, from detection to resolution.
3. Measure the impact with figures from the data, such as affected users, orders or minutes of downtime.
4. Identify the root cause and corrective actions, each with an owner and a date.

## Quality criteria
- The focus is on systems and processes, not on blaming people.
- Every figure comes from the available data.
- Actions are concrete and measurable.

## Limits
The report is a draft for review by the responsible team; do not assume unconfirmed causes and label them as hypotheses.
