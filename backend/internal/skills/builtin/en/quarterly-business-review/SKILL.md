# Quarterly business review
## When to use it
At the close of each quarter, to compare results with goals, understand the causes and agree the priorities for the next one.

## Steps
1. Compare the quarter's key indicators with the target, with the previous quarter and with the same quarter of the prior year.
2. Summarize achievements and misses; for each miss, state the confirmed cause or the hypothesis still to be validated.
3. Record the learnings: what worked, what did not and what the team will change.
4. Review the quarter's projects and commitments: finished, in progress, delayed or cancelled.
5. Propose three to five priorities for the next quarter, each with a target, an owner and the resources required.
6. Fill in the template `templates/quarterly-business-review.md` and schedule the session with area owners.

## Quality criteria
- Each target shows actual result, variance and data source.
- Causes distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses.
- Next quarter's priorities are few, measurable and have an owner.
- Prior-quarter commitments have an explicit status.

## Limits
This is a draft for leadership and the teams; it does not change targets, budgets or owners by itself. Do not attribute causes or figures that are not in the data, and do not share individual performance assessments outside the performance process.
