# Customer case study

## When to use it
To turn a real customer experience into sales or marketing material.

## Steps
1. Introduce the customer and its sector in two sentences, with permission confirmed.
2. Describe the initial challenge with concrete details.
3. Explain the solution applied and the implementation process.
4. Summarize measurable results, with figures and period taken from the data.
5. Include a customer quote and a final call to action.

## Quality criteria
- Each result has a verifiable figure or description.
- The story is brief, about five hundred words at most.
- The customer approves the name, quote and figures before publication.

## Limits
The case study is a draft that requires customer and company approval before it is published. Do not invent results, quotes or customer names.
