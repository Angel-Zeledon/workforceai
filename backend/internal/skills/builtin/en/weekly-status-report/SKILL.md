# Weekly status report
## When to use it
To keep leadership, the team or the client informed about a project's progress.

## Steps
1. Summarize the overall status in one sentence: on track, at risk or delayed.
2. List this week's progress with its result, not only the activity performed.
3. Flag blockers and risks with the proposed action and the person who owns it.
4. Close with next week's steps and pending decisions.

## Quality criteria
- Figures and dates come only from the available data.
- One page at most.
- Every blocker has an owner.

## Limits
The report is a draft for the project owner's review before sending; do not invent progress or deadlines.
