# NPS comment analysis

## When to use it

When closing an NPS survey period, to understand why customers rate as they do and what to change. It works with responses already collected.

## Steps

1. Count the responses by group: promoters (9 to 10), passives (7 to 8) and detractors (0 to 6). Calculate NPS as the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors, and show the number of responses in each group.
2. Confirm the sample size and response rate before comparing periods. If the sample is small, say so in the report.
3. Read the detractor and passive comments first and group them into themes with clear names, such as response times, price, ease of use or a missing feature.
4. Count how many comments mention each theme and which group they appear in. Highlight the themes that repeat most among detractors.
5. Extract from promoter comments the reasons that repeat, to know what to keep.
6. Propose two to four actions, each tied to a theme and to a metric that shows whether it works. Name an owner only if it is in the data.

## Quality criteria

- NPS is calculated with the stated formula and the responses per group are shown.
- Each theme has examples or figures, not just a label.
- Conclusions distinguish what the comments say from what is inferred.
- Actions are concrete and have a way to measure their effect.

## Limits

This is an internal analysis: comments are quoted without identifiable customer data. It does not attribute causes the comments do not mention, does not estimate NPS for periods without data and does not modify the survey.
