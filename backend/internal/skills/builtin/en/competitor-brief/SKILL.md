# Competitor brief

## When to use it
To prepare positioning, campaigns or sales conversations against a competitor.

## Steps
1. Identify the competitor, its target market and its main offer.
2. Summarize pricing and sales models only if they are public or in the data.
3. List three strengths and three weaknesses, each with its source.
4. Compare their offer with ours on features, price and experience.
5. Propose two or three messages that differentiate us without attacking them.

## Quality criteria
- Every claim cites its source or is marked as an assumption.
- The comparison is fair and does not overstate their weaknesses.
- Includes the date of the information analyzed.

## Limits
The brief uses only the information provided and does not replace legal or market research. Do not attribute to the competitor figures, customers or statements that have not been verified.
