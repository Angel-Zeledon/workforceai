# Payment reminder

## When to use it
To politely request payment of an overdue or soon-to-be-due invoice.

## Steps
1. Identify the invoice, the outstanding amount, the due date and the days overdue.
2. Pick the reminder level by delay: friendly, formal or final notice.
3. Write the message with the reference, the exact amount, the payment method and one single action requested.
4. Include the deadline to confirm payment and the contact for questions.

## Quality criteria
- The tone is firm but kind, with no accusations or threats.
- The amount and reference match the invoice exactly.
- If payment is already recorded as received, no reminder is produced.

## Limits
Leave the reminder as a draft for human approval before sending. Do not add fees, interest or deadlines that are not in the agreed terms.
