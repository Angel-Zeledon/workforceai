# Property listing

## When to use it
When publishing a property for sale or rent, to present it with verifiable facts and clear terms from the first contact.

## Steps
1. Gather the basic data: property type, transaction (sale or rent), area, size, bedrooms, bathrooms and condition.
2. List only verified features: utilities, amenities, orientation, year built, parking and available documentation. Leave out anything that is not confirmed.
3. Write a one-paragraph description that highlights two or three real points, with no superlatives or promises about appreciation or returns.
4. State the price or rent, what it includes, deposits, extra costs, payment method and how long the listing stays active.
5. Add the location with general references (neighborhood, nearby transport) and how to book a visit.
6. Fill in the template `templates/property-listing.md` and mark every pending field as "to be confirmed".

## Quality criteria
- Every published feature matches verified data about the property.
- The price, currency and extra costs are stated explicitly.
- The description has no empty superlatives or promises of gain.
- The listing does not disclose personal data of the owner or current occupant.

## Limits
This is a draft for review by the listing agent or owner before it is published. Do not publish the listing without human approval. Do not invent areas, measurements, age, permits or documents. Do not give legal advice or official valuations.
