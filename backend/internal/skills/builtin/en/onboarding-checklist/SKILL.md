# Onboarding checklist
## When to use it
To prepare a new hire's arrival and make sure nothing is left pending.

## Steps
1. Identify the role, team, start date and work mode.
2. Group tasks into before start, day one, first week and first month.
3. Assign each task an owner by role: HR, direct manager, IT or buddy.
4. Include access, equipment, paperwork, introductions and a 30-day follow-up meeting.

## Quality criteria
- Every task has a clear owner and deadline.
- Tasks follow a logical sequence.
- Only steps and facts found in the company information appear.

## Limits
Review the list with HR and the manager before sharing it; do not invent dates, access rights or policies.
