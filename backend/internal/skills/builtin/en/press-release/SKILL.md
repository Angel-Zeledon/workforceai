# Press release

## When to use it
To announce a launch, partnership, hire or milestone that is relevant to the media.

## Steps
1. Define the main news and why it matters to journalists.
2. Write a headline of at most twelve words with the key news.
3. Draft the lead paragraph with who, what, when, where and why.
4. Include a spokesperson quote and two context paragraphs ordered by importance.
5. Close with a note for editors and the media contact details.

## Quality criteria
- The first paragraph contains the complete news.
- The quote sounds natural and includes no unapproved facts.
- The style is objective, without exaggerated promotional adjectives.

## Limits
The release is a draft for communications and legal approval before distribution. Do not invent quotes, figures or dates; if data is missing, mark it as pending.
