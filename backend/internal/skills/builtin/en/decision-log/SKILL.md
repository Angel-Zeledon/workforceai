# Decision log
## When to use it
When a decision affects several areas or will have lasting consequences, and the team needs to remember why it was made and who made it.

## Steps
1. Write the context in two or three sentences: what problem prompted the decision and which constraints applied.
2. List the options considered, including doing nothing, with their main advantages and disadvantages.
3. Record the decision taken, its rationale and who made it, with their title or role.
4. Note the decision date, the actions it implies and the owner of each action.
5. Set the review date and the condition that would lead to reconsidering the decision.
6. Add the entry to the log using the template `templates/decision-log.csv`, one row per decision.

## Quality criteria
- Each decision states clearly who made it and when.
- At least two real options are recorded, not only the chosen one.
- The rationale mentions the criteria used, not only the conclusion.
- Every decision with a review date has a measurable condition for reopening it.

## Limits
The log documents decisions already made by people with authority; it does not decide or change agreements by itself. Do not record sensitive personal data (health, disciplinary matters, individual evaluations) or figures that are not in the sources.
