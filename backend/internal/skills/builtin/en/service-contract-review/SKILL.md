# Services agreement review

## When to use it
When a services agreement (client or supplier) must be reviewed and its risk summarized for a decision.

## Steps
1. Identify the parties, purpose, term, renewal and governing law.
2. Go through `references/clause-checklist.md` clause by clause.
3. For each finding give: clause, what it says, why it matters, risk (high, medium, low) and a proposed alternative wording.
4. Flag what is missing (for example liability cap, confidentiality, intellectual property, SLA).
5. Close with a 5-line summary and the recommendation: sign, negotiate or do not sign.

## Quality criteria
- Always quote the exact text of the clause.
- Separate facts of the contract from your opinions.
- Do not invent laws or case law; if you depend on local law, mark it as a point to confirm.

## Limits
This is not legal advice: a lawyer must validate before signing.
