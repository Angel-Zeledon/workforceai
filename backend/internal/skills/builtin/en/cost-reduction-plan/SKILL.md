# Cost reduction plan
## When to use it
When the company needs to recover margin or free up cash and leadership wants a plan with clear priorities, not across-the-board cuts.

## Steps
1. Set the baseline: spending for the last three to twelve months by category, with source and cut-off date.
2. Identify opportunities by category (suppliers, contracts, processes, consumption, staffing and space) and describe each in one sentence.
3. Estimate the annual saving of each opportunity with its assumption, its implementation cost and the time needed to achieve it.
4. Record the risks of each opportunity: impact on customers, quality, compliance or people.
5. Prioritize by net saving, ease and risk, and assign an owner and review date to each.
6. Fill in the template `templates/cost-reduction-plan.csv` and compare the total saving against the target.

## Quality criteria
- The estimated saving is calculated against the baseline and its assumption is written down.
- Each opportunity has an owner, a date and an identified risk.
- The plan separates confirmed savings from estimated savings.
- Reductions in staffing, contracts or compliance state the legal or contractual rule that governs them.

## Limits
This is a proposal: staff, contract or supplier reductions require human approval and legal review before they are carried out. Do not invent savings, prices or baselines; if a figure is missing, mark it "pending".
