# User research synthesis

## When to use it
To turn loose interview or survey notes into actionable findings.

## Steps
1. Review all notes and tag each observation with its participant or source.
2. Group observations into themes and count how many participants mention each one.
3. Pick one short, representative quote per theme, keeping participants anonymous.
4. Write findings with a concrete implication and separate what was observed from what was assumed.

## Quality criteria
- Each finding rests on at least two different sources.
- Quotes are verbatim and contain no identifiable personal data.
- The study limits are stated: sample size and participant profile.

## Limits
Leave the synthesis as a draft for the research team. Do not draw business conclusions when the data is insufficient.
