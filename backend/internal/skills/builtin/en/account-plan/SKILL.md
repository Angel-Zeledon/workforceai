# Key account plan

## When to use it
For strategic accounts that need a written growth and retention plan, at the start of a period or before a review with leadership. Use `templates/account-plan.md` as the structure.

## Steps
1. Summarize the current situation of the account: what it buys, since when, at what value, and how the relationship with the customer's team is going.
2. Map the contacts by role: decision maker, influencer, user and internal sponsor, noting the level of relationship with each and the last recorded interaction.
3. List growth opportunities with their estimated value, stage and what is needed to advance. Use only figures from the data.
4. Identify the main risks, such as competition, staff changes, budget or low usage, and separate observed facts from assumptions.
5. Define three to five next steps, each with an owner, a date and an expected result.
6. Set an objective for the quarter and the date of the next review with the team.

## Quality criteria
- Each contact has a role and a last interaction, or is marked as having no data.
- Each opportunity has a stage, an owner and a next step.
- Risks distinguish facts from assumptions and propose a response.
- Every next step has an owner and a date.

## Limits
The plan is internal and is not shared with the customer without review. It does not invent revenue, usage or satisfaction figures, or personal data about contacts. It does not change the CRM or send messages to contacts.
