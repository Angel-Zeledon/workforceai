# Quote builder

## When to use it
When a customer asks for a formal price proposal for a service or product.

## Steps
1. Summarize the agreed scope in one sentence and list the included deliverables.
2. Break down line items with quantity, unit price and amount.
3. Calculate subtotals, taxes and total, checking every operation.
4. Add payment terms, quote validity and exclusions.
5. Close with the next step to accept or request changes.

## Quality criteria
- Totals match the sum of the line items.
- Exclusions and assumptions are written out, not implied.
- Currency, taxes and validity period are stated explicitly.

## Limits
The quote is a draft for commercial and financial review before it is sent. Do not invent prices, discounts or tax rates that are not in the data.
