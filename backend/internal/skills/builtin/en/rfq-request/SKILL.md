# Request for quotation (RFQ)

## When to use it
When you are going to buy a good or service and need comparable offers from several suppliers before choosing.

## Steps
1. Define what is being bought, what for and how it will be used, in two or three sentences.
2. Write the specifications in measurable terms: dimensions, standards, service levels and technical characteristics. Avoid brand names unless there is a written justification.
3. State quantities, delivery frequency, delivery location, required lead time and the deadline to respond.
4. Declare the commercial conditions requested: currency, taxes shown separately, payment terms, offer validity, warranty and shipping cost.
5. Publish the evaluation criteria with their weights (for example price, technical compliance, lead time and experience), so everyone responds on the same basis.
6. Organize the response format so the offers are comparable (use `templates/rfq_request.md`) and check that there are no contradictory requirements before sending.

## Quality criteria
- All suppliers receive the same information, the same quantities and the same deadline.
- Each specification is verifiable: in an offer it can be confirmed as yes or no.
- The evaluation criteria and their weights appear before offers are received.
- The request states whether partial or alternative offers are accepted.

## Limits
This skill drafts the request. It does not send it, select suppliers or commit to conditions. Sending and awarding require approval from the person responsible for procurement. Do not include unauthorized reference prices or confidential data from other suppliers.
