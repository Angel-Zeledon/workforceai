# Privacy policy check

## When to use it
To verify that a privacy policy covers internal or regulatory requirements before it is published.

## Steps
1. Load the organization's requirements list or the applicable regulation named in the task.
2. Check whether the policy describes the data collected, the purpose and the legal basis of each processing activity.
3. Check retention periods, individual rights, transfers and the controller's contact details.
4. Deliver a table with each requirement, its status (met, partial or missing) and the suggested change.

## Quality criteria
- Each requirement marked missing or partial cites the section reviewed or states that it does not appear.
- Nothing is marked as met unless the text says so explicitly.
- The report separates what was verified from what needs technical confirmation.

## Limits
This review is a preliminary check and does not certify regulatory compliance. Do not invent contact details, periods or processing practices.
