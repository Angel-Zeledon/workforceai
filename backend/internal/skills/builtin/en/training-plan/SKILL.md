# Training plan
## When to use it
When a team needs to build specific skills and a plan is required, with objectives, content, dates and a way to measure progress.

## Steps
1. Start from the need: which performance or skills gap exists, backed by evidence such as assessments, repeated errors or team requests.
2. Define three to five measurable objectives phrased as "by the end, the person will be able to...".
3. Select content and formats (course, practice, mentoring or reading) for each objective, ordered from basic to advanced.
4. Build a schedule with weeks or dates, session length, owners and target audience.
5. Define the assessment: one observable practice task per objective and the date it is measured.
6. Fill in the template `templates/training-plan.md` and leave budget and vendors as pending if they are not in the information.

## Quality criteria
- Each objective has an observable assessment criterion.
- The schedule fits the audience's real availability.
- Each piece of content serves an objective; no topic is without purpose.
- The identified need is backed by a source.

## Limits
This is a draft: the budget, vendors and selection of participants are decided by the responsible person. Do not assess individuals or propose sanctions from this plan.
