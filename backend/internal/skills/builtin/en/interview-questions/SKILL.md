# Interview questions
## When to use it
To prepare an interview with a common script for every candidate of a role.

## Steps
1. Identify 3 to 5 key competencies from the job description.
2. Write one open question per competency, focused on concrete past experiences.
3. For each question, note what a strong answer looks like and which warning signs to watch for.
4. Include one situational question and, if the role requires it, one technical knowledge question.

## Quality criteria
- Every question relates to the work, not to personal life.
- No questions about age, marital status, family, religion or origin.
- The same script is used with all candidates for the role.

## Limits
This is a draft for the interviewer; do not assess or decide on candidates based on this document alone.
