# Complaint handling
## When to use it
To reply to an upset customer or a formal or informal claim.

## Steps
1. Identify the fact of the complaint, its impact on the customer, and any order or case reference.
2. Open with a specific apology for the concrete problem, without admitting fault that is not documented.
3. Explain what is known about the cause and the concrete action being taken, based on the available data.
4. Close with a follow-up commitment: who contacts, through which channel and within what timeframe.

## Quality criteria
- Empathetic and calm tone, not defensive.
- Every commitment appears in the task data.
- Legal language and excuses are avoided.

## Limits
Leave the reply as a draft for the owner's approval; do not offer compensation or refunds without authorization.
