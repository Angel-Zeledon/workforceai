# Marketing campaign plan

## When to use it
When a campaign with a defined objective, audience and period must be organized, before producing assets or buying media. Use `templates/campaign-plan.md` as the structure.

## Steps
1. Set a measurable objective and a deadline, for example how many demo requests are sought between two dates. If the figure is not in the data, leave it as pending.
2. Describe the target audience by segment, with its main problem and the buying stage it is in.
3. Define one core message in a sentence and two or three supporting messages, each with its proof or source.
4. Choose channels based on the audience and the objective, and assign each one its main asset and its role: reach, consideration or conversion.
5. Build the calendar by week with milestones for production, approval, launch and review, and the budget per channel with a reserve for adjustments.
6. Set the tracking metrics: one main, two supporting, and the threshold that would require changing the plan.

## Quality criteria
- The objective includes an action, a metric, a figure or criterion, and a date; objectives such as "increase visibility" without a measure are not accepted.
- Each channel has a reason tied to the audience, not just presence.
- The budget adds up to the stated total and separates production from media.
- The metrics can be obtained from the tools the company already uses.

## Limits
It is a plan for human review: it does not launch ads, publish assets or commit budget. It does not invent market figures, conversion rates, cost per click or results of past campaigns that are not in the data.
