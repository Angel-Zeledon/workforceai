# Escalation summary

## When to use it

When a case moves to a second level, to product, engineering or leadership, and the person receiving it must understand it without reading the whole thread. Use `templates/escalation-summary.md` as the structure.

## Steps

1. Identify the customer, their plan or account type and the channel they first contacted through, without including personal data that does not help resolve the case.
2. Write the history in chronological order, with the date and one line per relevant contact.
3. List what was already tried and the result of each attempt, so no one repeats the same steps.
4. Describe the impact with observable facts: what the customer cannot do, since when, how many users or operations it affects and whether there is a deadline.
5. Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses and mark each hypothesis as such.
6. End with the specific ask: what is needed from the receiving team, by when if the customer or the policy requires it, and who answers the customer in the meantime.

## Quality criteria

- The receiver can act without asking for basic details.
- Impact is expressed with figures or facts from the case, not adjectives.
- Each earlier attempt has its result.
- The ask is a concrete action, not a general phrase like "please help".

## Limits

The summary is internal and is not sent to the customer without review. It does not change the case status or assign owners who are not in the data. It does not promise the customer response times or solutions.
