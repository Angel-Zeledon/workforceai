# Budget vs actual

## When to use it
To measure budget compliance for a period and explain the variances.

## Steps
1. Align budget and actuals by the same line item, cost center and period.
2. Calculate the absolute variance (actual minus budget) and the percentage variance.
3. Sort the variances by size and flag those above the agreed threshold.
4. Write a short explanation for each relevant variance, with the known cause or the open question.

## Quality criteria
- Every figure in the report can be traced to the source data.
- Signs and percentages are consistent: a positive value means spending above budget.
- Causes are marked as confirmed or as hypotheses.

## Limits
Present the analysis as a draft for the person responsible for the budget. Do not attribute causes or figures that do not appear in the data.
