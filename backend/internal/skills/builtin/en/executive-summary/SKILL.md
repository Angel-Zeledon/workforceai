# Executive summary
## When to use it
When leadership must approve, decide or understand a topic quickly and needs a one-page read.

## Steps
1. Start with the decision: write in one sentence what leadership needs to approve or know, and by when.
2. Summarize the situation in three to five bullets with key figures and their source.
3. Present two or three options with cost, benefit and main risk for each, in a table if it helps.
4. Write the recommendation in one sentence, with the main reason and what will be stopped.
5. List next steps with owners and dates, and the risks that need attention.
6. Trim the text to one page and fill in the template `templates/executive-summary.md`.

## Quality criteria
- It can be understood without reading the appendices.
- Every figure cites its source or states that it is missing.
- The options are compared using the same criteria.
- The recommendation appears before the detail.

## Limits
This is a draft for the presenter; it does not make decisions or commit resources. Do not invent figures, deadlines or alternatives that were not discussed.
