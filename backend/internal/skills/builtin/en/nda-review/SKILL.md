# NDA review

## When to use it
To examine a confidentiality agreement before signing or negotiating it.

## Steps
1. Review the definition of confidential information and check that it is neither too broad nor too narrow.
2. Note the term, the usual exceptions and the return or destruction obligations.
3. Identify unusual clauses, such as disproportionate penalties, assignment of rights or an uncommon jurisdiction.
4. Propose concrete changes in a risk table with clause, risk level and suggested change.

## Quality criteria
- Each observation quotes the exact text of the clause reviewed.
- Risks are rated high, medium or low, with a short reason.
- Standard terms are distinguished from terms that need negotiation.

## Limits
This review is not legal advice; a person with legal competence must approve it. Do not fill in party details or dates that are not in the document.
