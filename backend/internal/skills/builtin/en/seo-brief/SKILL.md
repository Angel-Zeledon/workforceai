# SEO brief

## When to use it
Before drafting an article or page that must rank in search engines.

## Steps
1. Define the main keyword and two or three related variants.
2. Describe the search intent: informational, comparative or transactional.
3. Propose an SEO title and a meta description under 160 characters.
4. Structure the content into H2 and H3 headings with the questions it must answer.
5. List suggested internal links and the audience it addresses.

## Quality criteria
- The keyword appears in the title and one heading without being overused.
- The structure covers the full search intent.
- No metric is invented; if data is missing, say so.

## Limits
The brief is a guide for writers and human review; it does not guarantee search rankings. Do not include search volumes or competitor data that are not in the data.
