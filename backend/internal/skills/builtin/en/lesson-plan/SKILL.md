# Lesson plan
## When to use it
To prepare a class session with a clear purpose, a sequence of activities and a way to check what was learned.

## Steps
1. Define the grade or level, the subject, the session length and what the group already knows.
2. Write two to three learning objectives with observable verbs, such as explain, solve or compare.
3. Design the sequence: a 5 to 10 minute opening to activate prior knowledge, a development phase with guided practice and a closing synthesis.
4. Assign a time to each activity that adds up to the total length, and list the materials needed.
5. Include a question or task that shows whether each objective was met.
6. Fill in the template `templates/lesson-plan.md` and add adaptations for learners who need support or extra challenge.

## Quality criteria
- The timings add up to the session length.
- Each activity serves at least one objective.
- The assessment can be done during the session or in the next one.
- The materials are accessible to the whole group.

## Limits
This is a draft for the teacher, who decides the final content. Do not include students' personal data or assess students by name. Check the content against your institution's official curriculum.
