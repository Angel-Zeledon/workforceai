# Budget proposal
## When to use it
During the annual planning cycle, or when a team needs its own budget that leadership must approve.

## Steps
1. Define the period, currency, scope (company or area) and the objectives the budget must fund.
2. Document the main assumptions (volume, prices, salaries, exchange rate, inflation) with their source and owner.
3. Build the line items by expense type and, where applicable, by cost center, using the template `templates/budget-proposal.csv`.
4. Compare each line with the prior-year budget and actuals, and explain the variances that exceed the agreed threshold.
5. Separate fixed, variable and investment spending, and flag the items that depend on a pending decision.
6. Present the total, the assumptions and the cut or expansion alternatives so leadership can decide.

## Quality criteria
- Each line item has an assigned assumption, source and owner.
- Totals reconcile across line items, areas and the summary.
- Every variance against the prior year has an explanation or is marked as pending.
- The proposal shows at least one alternative scenario for the largest items.

## Limits
This is a proposal for leadership approval; it does not commit resources or issue purchase orders. Do not invent historical figures, rates or macroeconomic assumptions: use only the data provided and mark what is missing.
