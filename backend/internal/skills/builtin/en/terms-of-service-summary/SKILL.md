# Terms of service summary

## When to use it
To explain in plain language what a set of terms of service says.

## Steps
1. Read the full document and identify its main sections.
2. Summarize each area in one sentence: payments, permitted use, liability, cancellation, personal data and changes.
3. Highlight the user's obligations and any deadlines or penalties that may affect them.
4. Write the summary in plain language, with a section of points to review.

## Quality criteria
- No point in the summary contradicts the original text.
- Figures, deadlines and dates match the document exactly.
- Ambiguous clauses are flagged as such instead of interpreted.

## Limits
The summary does not replace the full legal text and is not legal advice. Do not add terms that are not in the document.
