# Tax checklist

## When to use it
To prepare a period's tax close without missing obligations or documents.

## Steps
1. List the period's obligations based on the taxpayer type and the jurisdiction given in the task.
2. For each obligation, state the required document, the responsible person and the deadline.
3. Mark which documents are already available and which still need to be collected.
4. Flag exceptions or doubts that need confirmation from a tax advisor.

## Quality criteria
- Each item has an owner, a document and a deadline, or is marked as pending.
- No rates, percentages or deadlines appear that are not in the data or in the stated jurisdiction.
- The list can be used as is for follow-up.

## Limits
The checklist is an organizational aid and does not replace a tax advisor's judgment. Do not state that an obligation does not apply without confirmation.
