# Board meeting pack
## When to use it
Before each board session, so directors arrive with the information they need to discuss and decide.

## Steps
1. Write a one-page summary with the three main messages of the period and the topics that require a decision.
2. Gather the key indicators with target, actual result, variance and source, always in the same format as the previous session.
3. Separate the topics for decision from the topics for information; for each decision include the recommendation and the options.
4. Add the main risks with their level, owner and the mitigation already under way.
5. Attach as appendices the financial statements, pending minutes and supporting documents, with version and date.
6. Fill in the template `templates/board-meeting-pack.md` and verify that every figure has a source and a cut-off date.

## Quality criteria
- Each decision topic states the exact decision requested and the recommendation.
- The figures in the summary match those in the appendices.
- Indicators show target and trend, not only the period value.
- The pack can be read in under an hour and understood without prior meetings.

## Limits
This is a draft for review by the chair or management; it is not sent to directors or third parties without human approval. It does not invent figures, minutes or approvals, and it does not replace review by finance or legal counsel.
