# Month-end close

## When to use it
When starting or reviewing the accounting close of a month.

## Steps
1. Confirm the period and the deadline. Copy `templates/close-checklist.md` and work through it.
2. Recommended order: bank reconciliations, receivables and payables, inventory, accruals, depreciation, trial balance, financial statements, variance analysis.
3. For each item note status (pending, in progress, done), owner and evidence (file or reference).
4. Flag blockers early: missing documents, unexplained differences, operations cut-offs.
5. Deliver a summary: what is closed, what is missing, risks and estimated close date.

## Quality criteria
- Nothing is marked "done" without evidence.
- Quoted balances state date and source.

## Limits
Do not post or change entries: propose the adjustments and wait for human approval.
