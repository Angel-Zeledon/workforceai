# Roadmap summary

## When to use it
To explain the product plan to leadership, internal areas or teams.

## Steps
1. Group initiatives by theme or strategic goal.
2. For each theme, state the priority, main milestone and current status.
3. Point out changes since the last review: added, delayed and removed items.
4. Close with the two or three most relevant risks or pending decisions.

## Quality criteria
- Each date or milestone appears as in the data and is presented as a plan, not a commitment.
- The summary fits on one page and reads well without prior context.
- Each priority has a short reason.

## Limits
Leave the summary as a draft for approval by the product owner. Do not change priorities or promise dates.
