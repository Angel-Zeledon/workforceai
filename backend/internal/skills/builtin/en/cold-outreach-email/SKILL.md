# Cold outreach email

## When to use it
To contact a prospect for the first time who has not asked for information.

## Steps
1. Identify one concrete reason the prospect matters: sector, challenge or news found in the task data.
2. Write a short subject line (at most 6 words) that sparks interest without overselling.
3. First paragraph: the prospect's problem and one sentence on how you address it.
4. Second paragraph: a brief proof point or case, only if it appears in the data.
5. Close with a low-friction question, such as a fifteen-minute call.

## Quality criteria
- Under 120 words.
- Names the prospect and their company, with no generic phrases.
- One value proposition and one call to action.

## Limits
Leave the email as a draft for a person to review and send. Do not invent clients, figures or results that are not in the data.
