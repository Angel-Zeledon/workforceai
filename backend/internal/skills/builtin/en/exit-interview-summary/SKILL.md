# Exit interview summary
## When to use it
To turn the notes from several exit interviews into themes, reasons and actions that HR or leadership can review.

## Steps
1. Gather the notes or transcripts from the interviews, removing names and any detail that identifies the person or their team.
2. Group each comment by theme: leadership, workload, compensation, development, culture, tools or other.
3. Count in how many interviews each theme appears, and separate recurring themes from isolated cases.
4. Summarize the main reasons for leaving with two or three short, anonymous quotes per theme.
5. Propose two to four actions, each with an owning department, a follow-up indicator and a review date.

## Quality criteria
- Each theme states how many people raised it, using numbers and not only adjectives.
- No quote allows a person to be identified, especially in small groups.
- Actions come from recurring themes, not from a single opinion.
- Reasons the person stated are kept separate from reasons that are inferred.

## Limits
Work only with the material provided; do not contact former employees or interpret personal motives. Conclusions and actions are a draft for HR review. Do not share summaries that include names.
