# Sales call preparation

## When to use it
Before a call or meeting with a prospect or an existing customer.

## Steps
1. Define the goal of the call and the minimum acceptable outcome.
2. Summarize the customer context: sector, current situation and the people attending.
3. Prepare three to five open questions to understand needs and budget.
4. Anticipate two or three likely objections and a short answer for each.
5. End with the proposed agenda and the next step to agree on.

## Quality criteria
- One page at most, readable in five minutes.
- Every customer fact cites its source or is marked as an assumption.
- States what must be decided by the end of the call.

## Limits
This is internal material for the salesperson; do not share it with the customer without review. Do not attribute to the customer information that is not in the task data.
