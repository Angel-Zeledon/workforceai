# SOP writer
## When to use it
To document a repeatable task so any team member can carry it out.

## Steps
1. Define the purpose of the procedure and who it applies to in one or two sentences.
2. List the materials, systems or access needed before starting.
3. Describe the steps in order, with one action verb per step and the expected result.
4. Add checkpoints, what to do when an error occurs, and who reviews the work.

## Quality criteria
- Every step is verifiable and understandable without prior context.
- The language is direct and imperative.
- No steps appear that are not in the source information.

## Limits
The procedure is a draft that the process owner must validate before publishing; do not invent policies or controls.
