# Shipping delay notice

## When to use it
When an order will not arrive on the promised date and the customer must know before the wait causes them a problem.

## Steps
1. Confirm the order, the promised date, the new estimated date and its source, and whether the new date is firm or only an estimate.
2. Explain the cause in plain language, without blaming the customer or revealing internal details, for example stock shortage, a carrier incident or customs review.
3. Present the available options: wait for the new date, receive a partial delivery, cancel at no charge, or receive a discount or credit, only if current policy allows it.
4. Write the apology as one concrete sentence without exaggeration, and the commitment to the next update with a date.
5. Adjust the tone to the channel and to the customer relationship, and keep the message under 150 words.
6. Deliver the draft with a subject line and in the customer's language, plus an internal note listing the data the approver must check.

## Quality criteria
- The new date is explicit and it is stated whether it is firm or estimated.
- The cause is explained without internal jargon or staff names.
- The options match current policy; nothing is promised that is not authorized.
- The message states when the next update will arrive.

## Limits
This is a draft. It does not send the notice, approve compensation, discounts or cancellations without authorization, and does not promise dates that operations has not confirmed. Any external communication requires human approval.
