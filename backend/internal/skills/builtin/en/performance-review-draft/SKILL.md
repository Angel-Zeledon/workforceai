# Performance review draft
## When to use it
To prepare a regular review backed by concrete evidence from the period.

## Steps
1. List the goals agreed for the period and their level of completion according to the data.
2. Summarize 2 to 3 concrete achievements supported by evidence such as projects, metrics or feedback.
3. Write 1 to 2 improvement areas as observable behaviors, not personal traits.
4. Propose 2 to 3 measurable and realistic objectives for the next period.

## Quality criteria
- Respectful and specific tone.
- Every statement has associated evidence.
- Achievements and improvement areas are balanced.

## Limits
This is a draft for the reviewer; do not assign ratings, raises or disciplinary decisions.
