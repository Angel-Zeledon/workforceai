# Demand letter

## When to use it
When an invoice, contract or agreement is not being honored and a formal written communication is needed before any other step.

## Steps
1. Confirm the details of the parties, the document that creates the obligation, and the amount or action required, with date and reference.
2. Describe the facts in chronological order, using only what is supported by documents: deliveries, invoices, emails and overdue dates.
3. State the breached obligation, citing the clause or the applicable rule. Check the rules on demands and default in your country.
4. Set a reasonable deadline with a specific date, and the expected result (payment, delivery or correction).
5. State the consequences the contract or the law allows, without threats that cannot be carried out, and reserve all rights.
6. Write the letter using `templates/demand-letter.md` and leave pending details marked in square brackets.

## Quality criteria
- Firm and respectful tone, with no insults, sarcasm or criminal threats.
- Every statement of fact refers to the document that supports it.
- Amounts, dates and references match the source documents.
- The letter asks for a specific action and a verifiable deadline.

## Limits
This is a draft. Sending it, setting binding deadlines or announcing legal action is decided by a person with legal competence. It does not include signatures or contact details that have not been provided.
