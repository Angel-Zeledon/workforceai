# Churn risk review

## When to use it
In the periodic portfolio review, or when a drop in usage, an overdue payment or a change of key contacts is detected at a customer.

## Steps
1. Gather the available signals for each customer: usage, support tickets, overdue payments, contact changes, upcoming renewals and competitor mentions.
2. Rate each signal as high, medium or low, and classify the customer as high, medium or low risk, explaining the rule you applied.
3. For each high or medium risk customer, propose the likely cause among price, adoption, product, service or a change on the customer's side, and mark whether it is a hypothesis or a confirmed fact.
4. Define a concrete retention action for each cause: a call, training, an adoption plan, or a proposal to review terms for approval.
5. Assign an owner and a date to each action, and the result that would show the risk has gone down.
6. Close with the list of customers without enough data to assess, to complete before the next review.

## Quality criteria
- Each risk rating cites the signals that justify it.
- The likely cause is kept separate from the confirmed cause.
- No action promises discounts or terms without approval.
- Customers without enough data appear in their own list and are never classified as low risk.

## Limits
It is an analysis for the account team. It does not contact the customer or change prices, contracts or CRM statuses. It does not invent usage metrics, satisfaction scores or churn reasons that are not in the data.
