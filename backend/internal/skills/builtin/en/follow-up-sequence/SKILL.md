# Follow-up sequence

## When to use it
When a prospect does not reply after a meeting, proposal or earlier email.

## Steps
1. Define the goal of the sequence and the total span in days.
2. Write the first follow-up as a short reminder that refers to the topic discussed.
3. In each later step add something new: a data point, a case or a question.
4. Vary the channel and tone, from friendly to direct, without pressure.
5. Include a final close that leaves the door open.

## Quality criteria
- Each message has a distinct subject line and a distinct contribution.
- No message exceeds eighty words.
- Proposed dates are consistent with the agreed timeframe.

## Limits
Deliver the sequence as drafts for human approval before any sends are scheduled. Do not add references, figures or commitments that are not in the data.
