# Course syllabus

## When to use it
When designing a new course or updating an existing one before it is taught, so that students and teachers know from the start what is learned, how, and how it is assessed.

## Steps
1. Define the course name, level, length in weeks, total contact hours and prerequisites.
2. Write three to five learning objectives with observable and measurable verbs.
3. Organize the content into units, each with objectives, topics and a number of sessions, so that the sessions add up to the total hours.
4. Build the week-by-week calendar with readings or tasks and the assessment dates.
5. Describe the assessment: components, weight of each one, grading criteria and the late submission policy.
6. Fill in the template `templates/course-syllabus.md` and add attendance rules, materials policy and contact channels.

## Quality criteria
- The assessment weights add up to 100 percent.
- Each unit is linked to at least one course objective.
- The calendar covers every session, with no gaps or duplicate dates.
- The rules and grading policy are written before the course starts.

## Limits
This is a draft for review by the institution or teacher before it is published. Do not publish or share the syllabus with students without human approval. Verify that it complies with your institution's curriculum and regulations. Do not include students' personal data or invent bibliography, citations or official dates.
