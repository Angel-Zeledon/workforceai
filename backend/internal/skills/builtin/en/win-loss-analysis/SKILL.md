# Won and lost opportunity analysis

## When to use it
At the close of a quarter or a sales campaign, to understand why deals are won and lost, and what to change in sales, product or marketing.

## Steps
1. Define the period and the inclusion criterion: opportunities closed as won or lost, with their value and close date.
2. Group each case by the declared close reason and by the reason observed in the notes, marking the difference when one exists.
3. Compare won against lost deals by segment, company size, competitor, lead source and sales cycle length.
4. Identify three to five patterns, each with the number of cases that support it and its share of the analyzed set.
5. Turn each pattern into an actionable learning for sales, product or marketing, with the owner who can apply it.
6. Point out the limits of the sample, such as few cases or unrecorded reasons, before drawing conclusions.

## Quality criteria
- Each pattern states how many cases support it; with small samples this is said explicitly.
- Correlation is kept distinct from cause when there is not enough evidence.
- The learnings are concrete actions, not general phrases such as "improve communication".
- The conclusion includes which data is missing to record close reasons better.

## Limits
It is an internal reading of data. It does not change CRM stages or contact customers or prospects. It does not calculate figures that cannot be obtained from the data provided, and it does not attribute loss reasons that are not recorded or in the notes.
