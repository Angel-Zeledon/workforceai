# FAQ builder
## When to use it
To turn repeated inquiries into a useful, organized help section.

## Steps
1. Group the inquiries received by topic, such as payments, shipping, account or usage.
2. Write each question using the same words customers use.
3. Answer in three to five sentences, starting with the direct answer.
4. Add a next step only if it exists in the available documentation.

## Quality criteria
- One idea per answer.
- No price, timeline or policy appears unless it is in the source.
- Questions are ordered from most to least frequent.

## Limits
Answers stay as drafts for team review; do not publish new policies or invent conditions.
