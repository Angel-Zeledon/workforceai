# Payroll control checklist

## When to use it
Before closing and approving each payroll run, to confirm that payments correspond to active people, approved hours and authorized items.

## Steps
1. Set the period, the pay frequency and the list of active staff at the cut-off. Compare the headcount with the prior period and explain the difference.
2. Review hires and leavers: each hire must have a contract and a registration record; each leaver must have an exit date and a calculated final settlement, and must not be paid after that date.
3. Review overtime, absences and sick leave against the attendance record approved by the direct manager. Flag any figure without support.
4. Review earnings and deductions: concepts and rates must match the current policy and the signed authorizations where they apply.
5. Compare the total net pay, employer contributions and withholdings with the prior period, and explain each variance above the threshold the company sets.
6. Reconcile the total to pay with the prepared transfer and, after payment, with the bank statement. Keep a record of the reconciliation.

## Quality criteria
- The number of people paid matches the active headcount for the period, plus hires and minus leavers.
- No leaver receives pay without an approved final settlement.
- Each overtime entry and each deduction has support and approval.
- Changes from the prior period are explained.

## Limits
This is a control checklist, not a tax calculation. It does not compute taxes or social contributions for any specific jurisdiction; verify the rules that apply in each country. It does not approve or transfer the payment, which is a person's decision. It works with internal identifiers and does not reproduce employees' personal data.
