# Newsletter draft

## When to use it
To prepare a regular edition of the email newsletter for subscribers.

## Steps
1. Define the central theme of the edition and the audience it targets.
2. Write a subject line and preheader that invite opening without overselling.
3. Draft a two-sentence intro that explains why the topic matters.
4. Develop two or three short sections, each with a headline and a clear takeaway.
5. Close with a single call to action and the sign-off given in the data.

## Quality criteria
- Each section reads in under one minute.
- Headlines are specific and do not repeat the subject line.
- All dates, figures and names come from the task.

## Limits
Leave the newsletter as a draft for editorial review before scheduling the send. Do not invent news, quotes or company facts.
