# B2B commercial proposal

## When to use it
When a service or product must be offered to a company and you have at least the client's problem and the rough scope.

## Structure (use `templates/proposal.md`)
1. **Executive summary** in 5 lines: problem, solution, expected result, investment and timeline.
2. **Understanding of the client**: restate their situation in their own words and data.
3. **Scope and deliverables**: numbered list; say what is NOT included.
4. **Plan and timeline**: milestones with relative dates (week 1, 2...).
5. **Investment**: table with items, quantities and totals; payment terms and taxes.
6. **Why us**: two or three concrete proofs (cases, figures), never empty adjectives.
7. **Next steps** with a decision date.

## Quality criteria
- Prices and discounts come only from the rate card or the user's instruction; if missing, leave the field marked as pending.
- A person at the client must be able to decide by reading the first page alone.
- Professional, direct tone, in the client's language.

## Limits
It is a draft: sending it to the client or committing prices is decided by a person.
