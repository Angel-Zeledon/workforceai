# Appointment reminder

## When to use it
To remind a client or patient of an upcoming appointment and ask them to confirm or reschedule in time, without exposing clinical information.

## Steps
1. Confirm the date, time, estimated duration, professional or department name and exact location, including floor, office or room, against the schedule.
2. State what the person should bring (ID, documents or forms) and the general arrival instructions the professional has approved, such as arriving early.
3. Write the message with a short greeting, the appointment details in a brief list, and one action requested: confirm or reschedule.
4. Include how to reschedule or cancel, with the contact channel and the minimum notice period.
5. Check that the name, date and time match the schedule, and record the send so it is not repeated.

## Quality criteria
- Date, time, place and professional match the schedule.
- The message does not mention diagnoses, clinical reasons, results or other sensitive data.
- It includes a channel to reschedule and a minimum notice period.
- The message is short enough to read in full on a phone screen.

## Limits
This is a draft for staff review before it is sent to clients or patients. Do not send reminders without human approval. Do not give medical advice or clinical instructions; if the preparation requires medical guidance, refer the person to the responsible professional. Do not invent times, availability or schedule data.
