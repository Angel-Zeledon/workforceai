# Support reply
## When to use it
To answer customer tickets or inquiries with a clear fix or status.

## Steps
1. Summarize the customer's request in one sentence to confirm it was understood.
2. State the fix or current status using only information from the ticket or the knowledge base.
3. If there are steps, list them in order, no more than five.
4. Close with the next step, who does it and how the customer can reach the team again.

## Quality criteria
- Friendly and clear tone, without unnecessary technical terms.
- No timelines, refunds or changes are promised unless they are in the data.
- Under 150 words unless the fix is long.

## Limits
Leave the reply as a draft for agent review; do not invent fixes, dates or policies.
