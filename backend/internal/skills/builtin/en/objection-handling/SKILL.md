# Objection handling

## When to use it
When a prospect raises a concern and you need a professional response.

## Steps
1. Identify the type of objection: price, timing, competition, authority or need.
2. Restate the objection in your own words to confirm you understood it.
3. Ask what sits behind the concern to uncover the real reason.
4. Answer with a concrete argument tied to their situation, using only available data.
5. Close with a question that brings the conversation back to the next step.

## Quality criteria
- Acknowledges the concern before countering it.
- Avoids defensive tone and pressure language.
- Each answer fits in three or four sentences.

## Limits
Deliver the answers as a support script for the salesperson, not as final text for the customer. Do not promise discounts, deadlines or results that are not authorized in the data.
