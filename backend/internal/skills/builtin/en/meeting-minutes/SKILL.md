# Meeting minutes
## When to use it
To put in writing what was agreed in a meeting and its follow-up.

## Steps
1. Record the date, time, attendees and the purpose of the meeting.
2. Summarize each topic discussed in one to three sentences, with the conclusion reached.
3. List agreements in a table with owner and due date.
4. Note open items that remain unresolved and the date of the next meeting.

## Quality criteria
- Only decisions that appear in the notes are recorded.
- Owners and deadlines are explicit.
- The minutes fit on one page.

## Limits
The minutes are a draft for attendee validation; do not attribute opinions or commitments that are not in the notes.
