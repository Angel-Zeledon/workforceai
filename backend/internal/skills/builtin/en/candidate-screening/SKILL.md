# Candidate screening
## When to use it
To review a set of resumes against the requirements of a vacancy.

## Steps
1. Take the must-have and nice-to-have requirements from the job description.
2. Compare each requirement with the experience written in the resume and quote the supporting evidence.
3. Mark each requirement as met, partially met, not met or not stated.
4. Summarize in one paragraph whether to advance, review or pass, and note the questions to ask in the interview.

## Quality criteria
- Every judgment is based on textual evidence from the resume.
- Age, origin, gender and personal situation are never inferred.
- Gaps are written as "not stated" rather than assumed.

## Limits
The final decision belongs to an HR person; do not contact or reject candidates based on this summary.
