# Landing page copy

## When to use it
Before publishing a page whose goal is for the visitor to take one specific action, such as requesting a demo or downloading a resource. Use `templates/landing-page.md` as the structure.

## Steps
1. Define the offer, the audience and the single action the visitor must take.
2. Write the headline with the main benefit, in fewer than twelve words, and a subheadline that explains who it is for and how it works.
3. Draft three to five benefits, each with the result for the customer and not only the product feature.
4. Include social proof only from testimonials, cases or figures that are in the data, with their source and permission; if there are none, leave the gap marked as pending.
5. Answer the two or three most likely objections in FAQs, and close with a call to action that restates the offer.
6. Check that each section carries one message and that the full text reads in under three minutes.

## Quality criteria
- The headline makes sense without context and names the benefit, not only the product name.
- Each benefit has a concrete result or is marked as a promise to validate.
- There is one call to action, repeated at the end of the page.
- No social proof sentence appears without a real source.

## Limits
Deliver the copy as a draft for marketing and legal review before publishing. It does not invent testimonials, customer logos, figures, certifications or guarantees, and it does not set prices that are not in the data.
