Data protection

What data the product stores, where it lives, how long it is kept, when it can reach an AI model provider and how to export or delete it. It is a technical description of the current state, verified in the code, not a legal policy.

Where the data lives#

Application data is in a Postgres database on a single shared virtual private server. Drive files are stored in the same database unless the operator configures S3-compatible object storage, which the public deployment does not use. Nightly backups are kept on the same server.

Each organization is isolated from the others with Postgres row-level security: logical isolation inside one database, not a database per customer.

Note

The server's location is not published on this page and a region cannot be chosen today.

How long it is kept#

Data deleted from the database may remain in backups for up to 14 days. Purging an organization keeps only a tombstone with the hash of its identifier, the reason and the date, with no name or personal data.

When data reaches an AI provider#

With real AI turned on, the task text, context, memory and relevant documents go in the prompt to the chosen model provider, after masking credentials and card numbers. Without a key, the product runs in simulation and nothing leaves. The current public deployment runs in simulation.

AI Workforce OS does not train models on your data. What each provider does with what it receives is governed by its own terms, which we have not audited. The full list is on the subprocessors page.

  • Leave the platform without an AI key so everything stays in simulation.
  • Limit the allowed providers in the organization's model policy.
  • Use your own AI key: while it is active, only that key answers and the platform keys are never used as a fallback.
  • Point to your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint, for example a local model.
  • Turn off prompt storage in traces if you do not want to keep prompts.

Export and delete an organization's data#

In Settings, Data & privacy, an admin or owner can download a ZIP with all of the organization's data: one file per table plus the drive files, with a manifest. Secrets (password hashes, ciphertexts, keys) are stripped inside the database before anything leaves it. One export per hour is allowed and it is recorded in the audit log.

From the same screen, an owner can delete the organization by typing its exact name and their password. The purge is scheduled 7 days later; until then the organization keeps working and any owner can cancel it.

  • Partial exports: audit log (JSONL, CSV or a ZIP with verification, for admins and owners), an agent's traces, artifacts, skills and usage report.
  • An admin or owner can delete conversations and remove members; anyone can leave an organization.

Your rights as a person#

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data. When your data is processed by a customer organization, that organization is the controller and we route the request through it.

There is no self-service export or deletion of an individual account today: your account is deleted when the last organization you belong to is purged. For any other request write to info@workforceai.es. We answer within the applicable legal deadlines, without a service-level commitment.

Trial accounts#

Trial accounts are disposable: they are created with their own organization and a generated password shown only once, they stop working after 24 hours and are purged automatically 7 days later. Do not use real or confidential data in a trial.

Legal documents and contact#

The privacy policy, the terms of service and the data processing agreement (DPA) are drafts pending review by a lawyer and are not published yet. Until then we cannot sign a DPA. For any data protection question write to info@workforceai.es.
