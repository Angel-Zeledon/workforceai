Data protection

This page explains what data the product stores, where it lives, how long it is kept today, how it can reach AI model providers and how to exercise your rights over it. It is a technical description of the current state, not a legal policy: the privacy policy and the data processing agreement are still under review by a lawyer and are not published.

Where the data lives

Application data is in a Postgres database on a shared virtual private server. The server's location is not verified on this page and a region cannot be chosen today. Nightly backups are kept on the same server. A file drive with S3-compatible object storage is optional and is not part of the public deployment described.

Each organization is isolated from the others with Postgres row-level security. This is logical isolation inside the same database.

Retention today

Today there is no per-organization configurable retention except for traces. Data deleted from the database may remain in backups for up to 14 days.

How data reaches AI providers

With real AI turned on, the task text, context, memory and relevant documents are sent in the prompt to the chosen model provider, after masking credentials, one-time codes and card numbers. Without a key, the product runs in simulation and nothing leaves. The providers the system recognizes are deepseek, anthropic, openai, kimi and custom; see the subprocessors page.

AI Workforce OS does not train models on your data. What each provider does with the data it receives is governed by its own terms, which we have not audited.

  1. Leave the platform without an AI key to keep everything in simulation.
  2. Limit the allowed providers in the organization's model policy.
  3. Use your own AI key and require that only that key is used.
  4. Use your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint, for example a local model.
  5. Turn off prompt storage in the organization's traces if you do not want to keep them.

Your rights and how to exercise them today

If you are a person whose data the product processes, you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. When your data is processed by a customer organization, that organization is the controller and we must route your request through it.

Today the channel is e-mail to info@workforceai.es. The endpoints for complete export and erasure of an organization or person are being built and do not exist yet. In the meantime there are partial exports for administrators: audit log (JSONL or CSV), an agent's traces, artifacts and skills, and an admin or owner can delete conversations and remove members. We try to answer within the applicable legal deadlines, without offering a service-level commitment.

Trial accounts

Trial accounts are disposable: they are created with their own organization and a generated password shown only once, and they stop working after 24 hours (login answers that the trial expired and session renewal is rejected). Do not use real or confidential data in a trial. An account that stops working does not mean its data was automatically deleted: today it is removed on request.

Legal documents and contact

The privacy policy, the terms of service and the data processing agreement (DPA) are drafts pending review by a lawyer and are not published yet. Until then we cannot offer a signed DPA. For any data protection question write to info@workforceai.es.
