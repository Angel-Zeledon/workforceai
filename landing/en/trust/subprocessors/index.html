Subprocessors

This page lists the third parties that may touch customer data, for what purpose and when. Where we have not verified something, such as a provider's location, we say it is not verified rather than assume it. The list is derived from the code and the deployment configuration in the repository; if they change, this page must be updated.

How to read the list

AI model providers receive data only when the organization, or the deployment operator, turns on real AI by configuring a key. Without a key, the runtime runs in simulation mode and no data goes to them. In the current public deployment, according to its configuration in the repository, the runtime is in simulation mode.

The deployment operator sets a ceiling of allowed providers (ALLOWED_PROVIDERS) and each organization can restrict its own list further. An excluded provider is neither called nor used as a fallback.

Services the customer connects

The available integrations are Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, LinkedIn and a custom API. When an organization connects them, data flows between the product and those services by the organization's decision and with its credentials, under its own agreements with them. We do not list them as our subprocessors because the customer chooses and contracts them. The Gmail integration works in simulation; with real accounts it is not yet tested.

The product's code and images are hosted on GitHub and its container registry, which receive no customer data. TLS certificates are issued by the certificate authority our proxy uses (Let's Encrypt, according to the deployment guide) and it only learns domain names.

How to restrict where data goes

  1. Configure no AI key: the product works in simulation and no data goes to model providers.
  2. Use the organization's model policy (Settings, model policy) to allow only the providers your internal policy accepts; anything not on the list is neither called nor used as a fallback.
  3. Bring your own AI key and require that only that key is used, so the platform does not fall back to its own keys.
  4. Point to your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint (for example a local model or a regional gateway) as the custom provider.
Tip. For organizations with data residency requirements: today we cannot guarantee a region. Contact us at info@workforceai.es before turning on real AI.

Changes to the list

We do not yet have a formal process for advance notice of subprocessor changes. The intention is to notify customers by e-mail with reasonable notice before adding one, and the draft data processing agreement (DPA), pending legal review, includes an authorization and objection mechanism. Until then, write to info@workforceai.es to receive notices.
