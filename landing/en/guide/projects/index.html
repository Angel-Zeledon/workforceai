Projects: big work, with follow-up

A short request is enough for a single task. When the work is big, use a project: it brings together objectives, tasks split among your employees, approvals and budget, and lets you see how much is left at any moment. What is a project and when should I use one?

A project is a plan with several objectives. Each objective is split into tasks, and an agent does each task. Some tasks are human steps: an approval or a milestone that only you can sign off.

Use one when the work has many parts, when several agents should work in parallel, or when you want to set a budget before starting. For something small, just ask in the chat.

How do I create a project?

  1. Open Projects and click New project.
  2. Write the Project goal in your own words, for example launching a campaign. Or pick a template in the Template selector; with No template (free plan) the plan is built from your goal.
  3. If you chose a template, fill in its parameters. On the right you get a preview of the plan: how many tasks it will have, how many human approvals, how much can run in parallel and an estimated cost.
  4. If you want, type a budget in USD. If you leave it empty, one is suggested from the estimate.
  5. Click Create draft.
Tip. Creating the draft costs none of your budget: the project has not started yet.

How do I review the plan before launching?

The draft shows you the Project plan: each objective with its tasks and the assigned agent. It is still yours to adjust.

  1. Change a task's title or the agent that does it. You can add acceptance criteria, one per line.
  2. Look at the Estimate: a likely cost (P50), a pessimistic one (P90) and the estimated duration.
  3. Under Quality review choose whether to review results: Do not review, Only if the agent is unsure or Always. Each review counts against the budget.
  4. Check that validation says everything is fine and enter the Approved budget. If it is below the likely cost, the app warns you and you must confirm the risk.
  5. Click Launch project.

How do I follow progress and stay in control?

Once the project is running, a bar counts finished tasks over the total. Check it first to see whether it is going as you expected. The cards in the list also show the status, pending approvals, failures and spend against budget.

Depending on the status you will see Pause, Resume or Cancel. Cancel asks for confirmation and stops the tasks that have not finished.

What does each project tab show?

The tabs follow a working order: first you spot, then you act.

  1. Health: tells you whether there is a problem.
  2. Map: shows you where it is.
  3. Kanban and Agent lanes: for acting on tasks.
  4. Timeline: for replanning.
  5. Delegation: who asked whom for work.
  6. Approvals: what you must authorize. Sensitive actions still ask for your go-ahead, as everywhere else in the app.
  7. Plan changes: adjustments proposed to the plan.
  8. Costs: what has been spent.

How do I start from a template or save my own?

  1. On the Projects list you will see the first templates. Click Use template to open the wizard with that template chosen, or See all for the full library.
  2. In the Template library you can search by name or description, use one, duplicate it to adapt it, or create one with New template.
  3. To repeat work you already did, open that project and click Save as template. The structure is kept; results and costs are not copied.

What happens when the project finishes?

If the project finishes successfully with at least two tasks done, your organization receives bonus coins. The bonus grows with the finished tasks, has a cap and is paid only once. A cancelled or failed project does not get it.

Coins are earned only by finished work, cannot be bought with money and are spent on cosmetic items in the shop. They do not change what agents can do.
