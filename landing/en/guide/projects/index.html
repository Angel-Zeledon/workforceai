Projects: big work, tracked

A request in the office bar is enough for a single task. When the work has many parts, use a project: it brings together objectives, tasks split among your agents, approvals and budget. Here you learn to create it, launch it, follow it to the end and repeat it with templates.

Decide whether you need a project#

A project is a plan with several objectives. Each objective splits into tasks done by an agent, and some are human steps, such as an approval or a milestone only you can sign off. It suits you when:

  • The work has many parts or several phases.
  • Several agents need to move forward in parallel.
  • You want to set a budget before starting.
  • You will repeat the same work and a template helps.

Tip

For something small, ask for it directly in the office bar.

Create a project#

  1. Open “Projects” in the side menu and press “New project”.
  2. Write the “Project goal” in your own words, or pick a template in “Template”. With “No template (free plan)”, the plan is built from your goal.
  3. If you picked a template, fill in its parameters and check the “Plan preview”: tasks, human gates, longest chain, parallel work and estimated cost.
  4. Enter a “Budget (USD, optional)” or leave it empty to have one suggested from the estimate.
  5. Press “Create draft”.

Tip

Creating the draft spends no budget: the project has not started yet.

Review the plan and launch it#

The draft shows the “Project plan”: each objective with its tasks and the assigned agent. Until you launch it, you can adjust it.

  1. Change a task's title or the agent doing it, and add acceptance criteria, one per line.
  2. Check the “Estimate”: the “Likely cost”, how high it could go and the likely duration.
  3. In “Quality review”, choose “Do not review”, “Only when the agent is unsure” or “Always”.
  4. Make sure “Validation” says the plan is valid.
  5. Enter the “Approved budget (USD)”. The app suggests the highest likely cost plus 10%.
  6. Press “Launch project”.

Note

Only tasks with acceptance criteria are reviewed, and each review counts toward the budget.

Warning

If the budget is below the likely cost, the project may pause for lack of funds; to launch anyway you must tick “I understand the risk and want to launch anyway”.

Provide the data the project needs#

Some projects need data to compute real figures. In that case the draft shows the “Before you start” card, and what you provide is stored in the project's Entradas folder, where all its agents read it.

  1. Press “Upload file” to upload the data from your computer.
  2. Or press “Pick from the drive” to use files already in Files.
  3. If you only want to try it, press “Use sample data”: fictional data that fills in what is missing.
  4. If you do not have the data, press “Launch without this data”: it is recorded and the agents will work with explicit assumptions.

Follow progress and control the project#

Each card in the Projects list shows the status, a bar with finished tasks over the total, how many “need you”, how many have problems and spending against the budget. Inside the project you see the same bar at the top.

Depending on its status, the project offers “Pause”, “Resume” or “Cancel”. Cancel asks for confirmation and stops any unfinished tasks. “Share with client” creates a progress link for your customer, and “Save as template” gets it ready to repeat.

Use the project's four tabs#

A launched project has four tabs. In the simple view you see “Tracking” and “Permissions”, and “More views” shows the other two.

If project approvals are waiting or an agent proposed changing the plan, “Tracking” says so at the top, with “Decide”, “See in Pending” or “Review changes”.

TabWhat it is for
TrackingSeeing what was done, what is in progress and the files each task produced
PlanSeeing the plan on the “Map” and, under “Other views”, changing how you look at it
CostsSeeing what has been spent
PermissionsDeciding what you must authorize and revoking what you left “Always allowed in this project”

Look at the plan another way#

The “Plan” tab opens the plan's “Map”. Under “Other views” you pick another way to see it, depending on what you need:

Tip

When there are plan changes to review, the “Plan” tab shows how many.

Start from a template or save your own#

All templates live in one library, inside Projects. There are two kinds: a “Quick request” goes to your agents right away, and a “Project” template is a bigger plan that you review before it starts.

  1. In “Projects”, look at the “Templates” card and press “Create one project” on the one you want, or “See all” to open the “Template library”.
  2. In the library, search with “Search by name or description” and filter by “Type of template”, “Source” or category.
  3. On a quick request, press “Use template”, fill in the required details and press “Start flow”.
  4. On a project template, press “Create one project”, “Duplicate” to adapt it or “New project template” to build one from scratch.
  5. To repeat a project you already ran, open it and press “Save as template”.

Note

Saving as a template keeps the project's structure; results and costs are not copied.

Tip

Quick requests come with the app: if you filter by “Mine and organization”, the library tells you so and offers “See all”.

Launch the same project for several customers#

With “Launch for several customers”, a project template becomes a batch: each customer gets its own project, with its folder, and one stuck customer does not hold the others back.

  1. Press “Launch for several customers” on a project template.
  2. In “1 · Customers”, choose the customers or press “Import a list” to paste rows from Excel or upload a CSV.
  3. In “2 · Work details”, write “What to do with each customer” and use the “Customer data” buttons so each project gets its own.
  4. In “3 · Review and launch”, check the “Estimated total cost”, set the “Budget per customer (USD)” and press “Launch for N customers”.

Note

The batch shows in the Projects list as a “Batch” card and counts as one active project of your plan.

Collect the bonus when it finishes#

When a project finishes successfully with at least two tasks done, your organization receives a one-time coin bonus. With the default settings it is 20 coins plus 2 per finished task, capped at 100. A cancelled or failed project does not get it.

Coins are only earned with finished work, cannot be bought with money, and are spent on cosmetic items in the Shop.
