Pending and security: you stay in control

Your AI agents prepare and propose, but the sensitive decisions are yours. Here you learn to decide your pending items, choose how much each agent asks you, review costs before running, set limits and permissions, and stop all activity with one click.

Understand what a pending item is#

A pending item is something that waits for your decision: an approval, when an agent wants to do something that needs your go-ahead, or a question, when it needs information from you. Each card says who asks, what it is, the details and the risk (“Low risk”, “Medium risk” or “High risk”). An agent can ask for approval, but never give it.

How much each agent asks you depends on its level. You choose it in its panel, “What it can do” tab, under “Permissions”; the level of new agents, in Settings, “Approvals”.

LevelWhat the agent does
Asks me about everythingAsks for your approval before every action other than reading or searching
Only what mattersDoes internal work on its own (reading, searching, drafts, internal files, analysis) and asks before anything that leaves the company or cannot be undone
Works aloneAsks nothing except the safety floor
Only suggests (advanced)Does nothing on its own: it proposes and a person decides

Note

The safety floor always asks, also in “Works alone”: payments, contracts and proposals, and deleting. Your organization's rules and spend limits apply at every level.

Decide in Pending#

“Pending”, in the side menu, brings together your agents' questions and the approvals of all your projects, one card at a time. It is the same card you see in the office tray and in the list view.

  1. Open “Pending”. The number next to it says how many are waiting for you.
  2. If you like, filter with “All”, “Requested by me” or “I can decide”, and under “Show:” pick “Everything” or “Emails to approve”.
  3. Read the card: who asks, what it wants to do, the details and the risk.
  4. Press “Approve” or “Reject”. If it is a question, write your answer and press “Answer”.
  5. If you made a mistake, press “Undo” while the decision has not been sent yet: it takes a few seconds and then it is final.

Note

Deciding requires the Admin or Owner role. If your role can only see the approval, the card tells you so and shows no buttons.

Tip

With the keyboard: A approves, R rejects and J/K move to the next or previous one; “View as a list” shows them all together.

Decide from your phone#

  1. Open app.workforceai.es/approvals in your phone's browser and sign in: you will see “Pending: approvals”.
  2. Press “Enable notifications” to find out when something is waiting for your decision.
  3. Open the approval and decide with “Approve” or “Reject”, or swipe right to approve and left to reject.

Note

The notification only shows the title and the risk; the decision is always made inside the app, with your session. On iPhone, add the app to the home screen first.

Tip

If an approval needs two different people, the card says so and shows how many approvals it has so far.

Stop approving the repetitive stuff#

If you approve the same thing again and again, the card offers to stop asking. It creates an exception you can undo; the safety floor and the company rules still ask.

  1. Press “Approve and do not ask again”.
  2. Choose who it applies to: the agent in all of its work, the agent only in this project or, if the request comes from a batch, everyone in every project of that batch.
  3. Press “Approve and remember”. At the top of Pending you will see what was created, with “Undo”.
  4. To review or remove exceptions later, open Settings, “Approvals”, and look at “Active exceptions”.

Note

For internal project actions that can be undone, the card also offers “Don't ask again for this kind of action”, for “This session (about 8 hours)” or “This project”, with “Approve for this session” or “Approve for this project”. You revoke them in the project's “Permissions” tab, under “Always allowed in this project”. Sending, publishing, paying, deleting and running code always ask for approval.

Review the cost before running#

Before running a request that may touch connections with write access, the office asks you to review its plan on the “Security and control” screen, under “Plan review”.

  1. Open “Settings” and go to “Safety and limits”.
  2. In “Plan review”, read the estimated cost (with its likely maximum), the approximate time, the “Connections it could touch” and the approvals expected.
  3. Take out any tasks you do not want with “Remove”.
  4. Press “Approve and run” or “Reject”.

Note

It is an estimate, not an invoice. If a request's maximum estimated cost goes over the confirmation threshold, the office also waits for your confirmation before starting.

Set spend limits#

  1. For a per-agent cap, open the Office's “List view”, go to “Costs & errors” and enter its “Monthly cap (USD)” under “Budget limits”. Press “Save”; leaving it empty removes the cap.
  2. To see every limit, open “Settings”, go to “Safety and limits” and scroll to “Spend limits”: each row shows the scope, period, usage, limit and what happens “When reached” (“Warn”, “Pause and ask” or “Block”).
  3. To limit a connection, open “Connections”, go to the “Usage and limits” tab, set “Uses per day”, “Writes per day” and “Monthly budget (USD)” under “Limits”, and press “Save limits”.

Tip

An agent that hits its cap shows as “Paused” under “Budget limits”.

Decide which connections each agent can use#

Connecting an account is not granting access: each permission is assigned separately, following least privilege. “Connections”, in the Teach and connect group of the menu, has three tabs: “Connections”, “Who can use what” and “Usage and limits”.

  1. Open “Connections” and press “Connect”.
  2. Follow the “Connect an account” wizard: pick “Other platform (API)” or “Database (read only)”, enter the “Connection name”, its details and its key and, if you like, use “Assign to agents (optional)”.
  3. Open the “Who can use what” tab. In “Permissions per agent”, click a cell (agent and connection) to view or change its permissions; “What each agent can do” sums it up per agent.
  4. Check the “Usage log”, in the “Usage and limits” tab, to see what happened.

Note

In this version connections are generic and read only: a system with an API and a key, a PostgreSQL database, a data inbox or a link that returns data. Integrations with specific products, such as e-mail, calendar or chat, are not available.

Protect your keys#

Your connections' keys and secrets are sent once and stored encrypted on the server. The screen never shows them again: to change one, you rotate it.

The AI model does not see them either, because tools are run by the server, not the model. With a real model, task text does travel to the configured model provider, so check its terms before using sensitive data.

Note

AI Workforce OS does not hold any security certifications today.

Use the security center#

The “Security and control” screen (Settings, “Safety and limits”) gathers the office's brakes. Nothing you do here deletes data.

ControlWhat it does
Global read-only modeAgents keep reading and proposing, but send and change nothing
Pause agentsA paused agent starts no new tasks and uses no connections, and resumes where it left off
Stop by toolSwitches a tool off for everyone; any admin stops it and only the Owner turns it back on
Operating hoursOutside hours, new work waits in plain sight; approvals and the emergency stop keep working
Unusual activity alertsWarn about a spend spike, bursts of tool requests, many rejected approvals or new recipient domains; they never block on their own

Tip

Alerts can freeze the office automatically, but that option is off by default.

Stop everything in an emergency#

“Stop everything” is in Settings, in your avatar menu and in the security center. Any admin can stop, but only the Owner can lift the lock. Nothing is deleted. There are two levels:

  1. Press “Stop everything” and choose the level.
  2. Write a reason if you like and confirm with “Yes, stop everything”.
  3. To resume, the Owner opens “Safety and limits”, writes the “Reason to resume” and presses “Lift lock”.
LevelWhat it does
Everything stoppedNothing new starts and connection use is blocked; accounts stay connected
Full lockdownAlso suspends all connections and cancels pending sends

Note

When you resume, actions that were on hold ask for approval again.
