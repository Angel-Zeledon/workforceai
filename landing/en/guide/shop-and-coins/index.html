Coins and the Shop: decorate your office with what your team earns

Every time your team of agents finishes work, your organization earns coins. In the Shop you spend them on items for the characters and for the office. It is a fun part of the app, and it is purely cosmetic.

How do I earn coins?

Coins are earned only by finished work. There is no way to buy them with money, or to pay to get more.

Every new account starts with 1000 welcome coins, so you can try the Shop from the first minute. After that, your balance goes up when an agent finishes a task or when a project is completed.

Tip. Your balance is shown at the top of the Shop screen, next to the totals you have earned and spent.

How much do tasks and projects pay?

With the default settings, each task pays by size: S pays 5 coins, M pays 10, L pays 20 and XL pays 40. A task with no size set counts as M.

If a quality review approves the result on the first try, the task pays 50% extra. If the review asked for rework and the task finished afterwards, it pays half of its base value.

When a project finishes successfully, it pays a completion bonus: 20 coins plus 2 for each finished task, capped at 100. The project needs at least 2 finished tasks to get it.

Nothing is paid for tasks that failed, were skipped, were cancelled or got blocked, for tasks with a rejected approval, or for milestones and human steps, because no agent did any work there.

How do I find an item in the Shop?

The Shop has several hundred items, so filtering helps. They are always sorted from cheapest to most expensive.

  1. Open Shop from the app menu.
  2. Pick a group: Characters for your agents' outfits and accessories, or Office to decorate the space.
  3. Click a category to see only that one. Under Characters you will find Outfits, Hats, Glasses, Headphones, Neckwear, Badges and Companions. Under Office you will find Plants, Lamps, Rugs, Coffee machine, Posters, Bookshelves, Aquariums, Arcade, Trophies and Views.
  4. Click All to see the whole group again. If a list is long, use Show more to load the rest.
Tip. When you switch group, the categories go back to All.

How do I buy an item?

Each card shows a preview, the rarity (Common, Rare or Epic), a description and the price.

  1. Check your coin balance at the top of the screen.
  2. Find an item you like and press Buy.
  3. If it costs more than you have, the button is disabled and the card tells you how many coins you are missing.
  4. After you buy it, the item goes to your inventory. It is not applied by itself: the card shows In your inventory and a link to Equip from inventory.

How do I use what I bought?

Everything you buy lives in the Inventory tab. From there you decide who wears it or where it goes.

  1. Open the Inventory tab.
  2. For a character item, choose the agent in the Character selector and press Equip. To undo it, press Take off. You will see who wears it under Worn by.
  3. For an office item, press Place and it appears in your office. Press Remove to take it out.
  4. Go back to the office to see the result in 3D.
Tip. If you already own an item, the Shop marks it as You own it.

Where can I see when my team earned or spent coins?

Open the History tab. It is a list with when it happened, what happened, which agent earned it, how many coins, and what your balance was after that.

Each line states the reason: welcome coins, task finished, task approved on the first try, task finished after rework, project finished or a purchase. There is also a summary of coins earned by agent.

Do items change what my agents do?

No. Items are cosmetic only. A hat or a lamp makes the office look better, but it does not change what an agent can do, its permissions or the quality of its work.

Think of coins as recognition for finished work, not as a way to give agents more power.
