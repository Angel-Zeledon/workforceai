Settings: configure your office in one place

Settings brings together your office's configuration, its security and your account on one screen. Here you learn what each card does and how to use the ones you will need most.

Find your way around Settings#

Open “Settings” at the bottom of the side menu. You will see cards grouped into Workspace, Safety and Account; some appear dimmed if your role cannot use them.

  1. Press a card to open its page. At the top you will see a path like “Settings > Office settings”.
  2. To go back, use the arrow in the path or your browser's Back button.

Tip

The “?” button in the top bar opens a guided tour of this screen.

Customize the office#

  1. Open “Office settings”.
  2. In “Language”, choose Español, English or Português for the interface.
  3. In “Labels”, turn “Show names” and “Activity bubbles” on or off, and adjust the “Label size”.
  4. Turn on “Reduce animations” if you prefer less motion.
  5. In “Employees”, change each one's name, nickname, job title, color, skin tone, hairstyle or accessory.

Hire an employee#

“Hire from template” adds an AI employee from a role template. The new agent starts with minimal autonomy and can never approve on its own. Only admins can use it.

  1. Open “Hire from template”.
  2. Pick a profession from the list.
  3. Type a name for the new agent, or leave it empty since it is optional.
  4. Press “Hire”.

Tip

If the office is full or an agent with that name already exists, the app tells you and does not hire.

Put a ready-made flow to work#

“Templates” opens the “Workflow template gallery”: flows with dependencies and parallel steps that your team runs while you approve what matters.

  1. Open “Templates”.
  2. Filter by category, for example “Sales” or “Finance”, or leave “All”.
  3. Review the steps of the template you are interested in and press “Use template”.
  4. Fill in the required details and press “Start flow”.
  5. Follow the progress in the office.

Describe your company and choose the AI model#

If your workspace supports it, you will see the “Set up your office” card until you complete it, and “Team settings” afterwards.

  1. Open “Set up your office” and go through its steps: “Business type”, “Language and tone” and “Daily briefing”. Press “Finish”.
  2. Later, in “Team settings”, change the “Regional tone” of the office or of each agent and schedule the “Daily briefing”.
  3. In “AI model”, use the included model or press “Use my own key” to connect your OpenAI, Claude, Kimi, DeepSeek or other compatible account.
  4. Paste your “API key”, press “Test connection” and then “Save”.

Note

If the server has no model configured, the AI runs in simulation mode, with scripted answers. Your key is stored encrypted and is never shown again.

Warning

With your own key, the provider bills you directly for what your agents use.

Connect tools and review security#

“Connections” is where you link email, calendar and other tools so agents can work with your data. Connecting is not granting: permissions are assigned per employee.

“Security” gathers permissions, spend limits, plan review, operating hours and the office's brakes. “Stop everything” immediately stops all external activity by every agent: only an admin can turn it on and, if it is already on, the card shows the level and takes you to Security.

Tip

The step-by-step for permissions, limits and brakes is in the approvals and security guide.

Protect your account#

  1. Open “Account security” and press “Turn on” under “Two-step verification”.
  2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, enter the code it shows and press “Confirm and turn on”.
  3. Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe and press “I've saved them”.
  4. If you lost a device, press “Sign out everywhere”.

Note

Admins can set up single sign-on with Okta, Entra ID or Google, verify domains, require MFA and provision users with SCIM from “Single sign-on and identity”.

Warning

Recovery codes are not shown again: without them and without your phone you will not be able to sign in.

Export your data or delete the organization#

  1. Open “Data & privacy”.
  2. Press “Export my organization's data” to download a ZIP with all your data and the drive's files. It never contains secrets, and you can generate one export per hour.
  3. To close the account, press “Delete my organization”, type the organization's name and your password, and confirm.
  4. If you change your mind, press “Cancel the deletion” before the period ends.

Note

Exporting is available to the Owner and admins; deleting, only to the Owner.

Warning

The organization is permanently deleted 7 days after you confirm, with its agents, conversations, projects, files, connections and members, and no copy is kept. Export anything you want to keep first.

Invite people and manage roles#

In the Account group you see the name, email and role you signed in with. Agents can never approve, whatever the role. These are the roles for people:

  1. Open “Members and invitations”.
  2. In “Invite someone”, enter the email, choose the role and press “Create invitation”.
  3. Copy the link and share it with the person: it is shown only once and expires in 7 days.
  4. Change a member's role, remove them or revoke a pending invitation from the same screen.
RoleWhat it can do
Read onlySee the office, chats, files, skills and the Shop, without changing anything
MemberAll of the above, plus ask for work, write in chats, upload files, create skills and spend coins
AdminAll of the above, plus decide approvals, hire agents, manage connections and permissions, approve skills, invite people and stop everything
OwnerEverything above, plus lift emergency locks, delete the organization and assign any role

Note

An admin can only invite and manage Members and Read only users; the Owner can assign any role.
