Settings: configure your office in one place

Settings gathers on one screen everything that used to hide in the gear menu. Each card opens its area as a page of its own, with a breadcrumb at the top.

How do I get around Settings?

Open Settings from the sidebar. You will see cards grouped into Workspace, Security and Account, each with a line explaining what it is for.

When you press a card, that area opens as a page and a breadcrumb such as Settings > Office settings appears at the top. There is no Close button.

  1. Press a card to open its page.
  2. To go back, use the back arrow in the breadcrumb or your browser's Back button.
  3. You can also click another section in the sidebar and you will switch screens directly.
Tip. If you want a guided visit, the ? button in the top bar opens a tour of this screen.

How do I customize the office?

The Office settings card makes the office look and speak your way. It is worth doing first.

  1. Open Office settings.
  2. Under Language, pick the interface language.
  3. Under Labels, turn Show names and Activity bubbles on or off, and adjust the Label size.
  4. Turn on Reduce animations if you prefer less motion.
  5. Under Employees, customize each employee separately.

How do I hire an employee from a template?

Hire from template adds a new AI employee from a role template. The new agent starts with minimal autonomy and can never approve on its own.

Only admins can use it; if the card looks dimmed, your role does not allow it.

  1. Open the Hire from template card.
  2. Pick a profession from the list.
  3. Type a name for the new agent, or leave it empty because it is optional.
  4. Press Hire. If the office is full or an agent with that name already exists, you will see a notice.

How do I put a ready-made team to work?

The Templates gallery offers ready workflows, with dependencies and steps that run in parallel. It is the fastest route if you would rather not build the team from scratch, and you still approve what matters.

  1. Open the Templates card.
  2. Filter by category: All, Sales, Finance or Assistant.
  3. Review the steps and the execution order of the template you like and press Use template.
  4. Fill in the required fields and press Start workflow.
  5. Follow the progress in the office.

Where do I connect tools and review security?

Connections is where you link email, calendar and other tools so agents can work with your data. Connecting is not granting: permissions are assigned per employee.

Security gathers permissions, spend limits, operating hours and the office's brakes. Review it before letting agents act on their own.

The Stop everything card immediately halts all external activity by every agent. Only an admin can trigger it; if it is already active, it shows the level and takes you to Security. For the details, read the approvals and security guide.

Tip. Real integrations are still being tested; see the Current status section of the site.

How do I describe my company so the team knows it?

If your workspace supports it, you will see an initial setup card. While it is pending it is called Set up your office: you pick your business type and get your team ready in a minute. Afterwards it appears as Team settings, where you can change the regional tone and schedule a daily briefing.

The goal is for the team to work with your company's context.

How do I invite people and manage my account?

In the Account group you see the name, email and role you are signed in with. The Members and invitations card appears if your role allows it. The roles are Owner, Admin, Member and Read only, and agents can never approve, whatever the role.

  1. Open Members and invitations.
  2. Under Invite someone, choose the role and press Create invitation.
  3. Copy the link and share it with the person. It is shown only once and expires in 7 days.
  4. Change a member's role, remove them or revoke a pending invitation from the same list.
  5. When you finish, press Log out to end the session on this device.
