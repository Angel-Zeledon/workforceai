The office: 3D view, list view and your agents

The Office has two views of the same work: the “3D view”, to ask for work and watch your agents, and the “List view”, to review numbers and tables. Here you learn to move around the app, talk to each agent, hire new ones and make the office your own.

Find your way around the menu#

The side menu groups the screens by what you use them for. On a phone it opens with the menu button in the top bar.

At the bottom are “Shop”, “Settings” and the “See everything” switch. Off is the simple view: advanced things are folded behind a “More views” button, one click away. On, every view is open across the app. It is remembered per person.

GroupScreens
Your dayOffice, Pending, Chats and Projects
Your companyAgents and offices, Customers, Files and Meetings
Teach and connectSkills and Connections

Tip

Next to many titles you will see “What is this?”: it explains in two lines what a request, a pending item, a template and other concepts are.

Move the camera#

The 3D view is a scene and you choose the point of view. While no agent is selected, the bottom right corner reminds you of the mouse gestures.

ActionWith a mouseWith a phone held upright
Rotate the viewDrag with the left buttonTwist two fingers on the screen
Zoom in or outUse the scroll wheelPinch with two fingers
Pan the viewHold the right button and dragDrag with one finger

Tip

Get close to a desk to see better what each agent is doing.

Understand what your agents are doing#

Each person in the scene is an agent, and what it does reflects its real work: typing at its desk, meeting others to plan, or waiting in the approvals area when it needs your permission.

Its status shows in its panel and on the “Agents” cards of the list view: “Free”, “In progress”, “Waiting”, “Needs you · an approval”, “Needs you · a question”, “Has a problem” or “Done”.

Talk to an agent#

To talk to one specific agent instead of the whole office, use its panel. It has five tabs; in the simple view you see “Chat” and “Work”, and “More” shows the rest.

  1. Click the agent in the scene.
  2. Write to it in the “Chat” tab of the panel that opens on the right.
  3. Go through the other tabs to see its work, what it can do, what it knows and its profile.
  4. Close the panel to see the whole office again.
TabWhat you find
ChatYour direct conversation with the agent
WorkIts status, tasks, reports, files and activity; folded, “How it thought” and “With other agents”
What it can do“Pause or try without sending”, its permissions, its folders and its “Tools and connections”
What it knowsIts skills and its memory
ProfileWho it is, its job, its “Appearance” and its quality

Tip

In “Work”, a finished task offers “Save as skill” so you can reuse that method.

Follow activity and pending items#

At the top left is the “Live activity” box, with what each agent does step by step. In the simple view it starts minimized: open it with its arrow and minimize it again whenever you want.

At the bottom left, the “Pending approvals” tray appears when something is waiting for you. You can decide right there with “Approve” or “Reject”, or press “Decide all N one by one” to go to “Pending”. In the scene, the agent waits in the approvals area.

If you were away for a while, the “While you were away” button at the top right sums up what happened.

Tip

On small screens the activity box does not appear; use the list view to follow it.

Adjust the light#

The “Light” button at the top right changes the lighting of the whole office. Changes are saved right away.

  1. Press “Light”.
  2. Pick a mood: “Natural”, “Warm”, “Cool”, “Sunset” or “Night”.
  3. Adjust the “Temperature” slider, from warm to cool, and the “Brightness” slider, from dim to bright.
  4. Close the panel with the X.

Tip

The light is visual only: it changes nothing about the agents' work.

Redecorate the office#

With “Edit office”, next to “Light”, you place the furniture you bought in the Shop and change the ambience. You need a role that can spend coins (Member or above).

  1. Press “Edit office”.
  2. In “Furniture”, pick a piece from “Your furniture” and click in the office to place it. The R key rotates it and Esc cancels.
  3. Drag anything already placed to move it; Del removes it.
  4. In “Workstations”, choose the style of the desks and chairs.
  5. In “Ambience”, change walls, floor, lighting and the view from the window.
  6. Press “Save” to keep your changes or “Discard” to leave without saving them.

Tip

If someone else changed the office while you were editing, press “Reload” to see their changes.

Switch to the list view#

The “3D view / List view” switch in the top bar changes the view without leaving the Office. The list view shows the same work as numbers and tables, with these sections on the left:

  1. Press “List view” in the top bar.
  2. Start with “Overview”: “Active tasks”, “Tasks done”, “Cost” (with its share of the budget), “Errors” and “Active agents”.
  3. Use the “Go to” cards to jump to “Customers”, “Requests” (in Chats), “Pending” or “Files”, which have a screen of their own.
  4. Press “3D view” to ask for work again and watch the agents.
SectionWhat you find
OverviewIndicators, the “Go to” cards, your agents with their status, pending approvals, live activity and the decisions made
AgentsOne card per agent with its done and pending tasks and what it can do without asking you, plus the conversations between agents
TasksEvery task and its status
QualityAn advisory score per agent, which never changes what it can do without asking you nor its permissions
Costs & errorsThe budget, cost per agent and per request, “Budget limits”, and errors and blocks
ReportsThe reports of completed requests

Note

In the simple view, “Quality”, “Costs & errors” and “Reports” sit behind “More views”.

Tip

If the “Pending” card or the “Errors” indicator change color, they are worth a look.

Hire agents and organize your offices#

“Agents and offices”, in the Your company group of the menu, brings together your agents and how they are organized, in four tabs:

  1. Open “Agents and offices” and stay on the “Agents” tab.
  2. Press “Hire an agent” and pick a role from the list.
  3. Type a name for the new agent, or leave the “Name (optional)” field empty.
  4. Press “Hire”.
TabWhat it is for
AgentsEvery agent you can see, whose it is and who uses it; “Hire an agent” and each agent's job description
OfficesEach office is a group of people and agents with its memory; the ones with their own space have their place in the 3D view
Org chartWho reports to whom
Delegation rulesWho may assign work to whom and who approves it

Note

Only an administrator can hire. The new agent can do very little without asking you at first, and it can never approve on its own.

Tip

If the office is full or an agent with that name already exists, the app tells you and does not hire.

Read the top bar#

The top bar stays with you on every screen. Depending on the width of your screen, it shows:

  • “Active”, “Pending” and “Cost”, on very wide screens; the Pending number changes color when there is something.
  • The “Daily goal”, for example “Finish 1 task today”, with its progress and its coin reward.
  • “Getting started”, while you have steps left on the starter list.
  • The “?” button, which opens a guided tour of the current screen.
  • Your coins: the balance; press it to open the Shop.
  • “Live” or “Reconnecting…”: the connection status.
  • Your avatar, with “My account”, “Stop everything” and “Sign out”.

Note

If the AI runs in simulation mode, there is no real model behind it and the cost you see is not a real charge from a provider.
