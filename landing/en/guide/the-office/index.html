The 3D office and the Dashboard

Your team of agents works in two views of the same system: the 3D office, to watch and ask, and the Dashboard, to review numbers and tables. This guide shows you how to move around both.

How do I move the office camera?

The office is a 3D scene and you control the point of view with the mouse. In the bottom right corner you will see a reminder of the gestures while no employee is selected.

  1. Drag with the left button to rotate the scene.
  2. Use the mouse wheel to zoom in or out.
  3. Hold the right button and drag to pan the view sideways.
Tip. Get close to a desk to see better what each agent is doing.

What are the moving employees doing?

Each person is an agent and their behavior reflects their real work in the system: typing at their desk, meeting others to plan, or waiting at the approvals desk when they need your permission.

You do not have to program or watch them. You ask for something in the bar at the bottom and the office splits the work.

How do I open an employee's panel?

If you want to talk to one specific agent instead of the whole office, use their panel.

  1. Click the employee in the scene.
  2. Their panel opens on the right, with their work, their files and a direct conversation with them.
  3. Type there whatever you want to ask or request.
  4. When you are done, close the panel to see the whole office again.
Tip. You can also get here from the Dashboard: clicking an agent's card takes you back to the office with that agent selected.

How do I follow activity and approvals from the office?

At the top left is the "Live activity" box: it shows tasks starting, files being touched and messages between agents. It is the quickest way to confirm your request is moving.

At the bottom left, the approvals zone appears when something is pending. An agent that needs your permission waits at the approvals desk, and there you can press Approve or Reject.

Tip. The activity box is hidden on small screens; in that case, use the Dashboard.

How do I change the lighting?

In the top right corner of the office is the "Office lighting" control. It has three modes:

  1. Ambient: the scene's general light.
  2. Lights on: a lit office, like daytime.
  3. Night: a dark mood, more comfortable if you work in low light.
Tip. It is visual only. It changes nothing about the agents' work.

How do I switch to the Dashboard and what does it show?

In the top bar, the Office / Dashboard toggle takes you from one view to the other. The Dashboard is the same operation, but as numbers and tables. On the left it has sections: Overview, Requests & plan, Tasks, Agents, Conversations, Approvals, Reports, Files and Costs & errors.

Overview shows indicator cards: Requests, Active tasks, Tasks done, Approvals, Cost with its percentage of the budget, Errors and Active agents. If cost or errors change color, it is worth a look. Below is the Team, with one card per agent, its status and what it is doing now (View all opens the Agents section), and beside it the pending approvals and live activity.

  1. Press Dashboard in the top bar.
  2. Start with Overview and review the indicators.
  3. Open the section you need, for example Approvals or Reports.
  4. Press Office when you want to ask for things again and watch the agents move.

What do the numbers in the top bar mean?

The top bar stays with you on every screen. It shows pending Approvals (the number changes color when there are any), accumulated Cost and a Coins counter. Coins are earned by finished work, are not money and cannot be bought. You will also see whether the connection is Live or Reconnecting.

While the AI runs in simulation, there is no real model behind it, so there is no real spending attached.
