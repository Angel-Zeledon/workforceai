Getting started: your first 5 minutes

AI Workforce OS is a 3D virtual office where the employees are AI agents. You ask for what you need in your own words, they split the work, and you decide the important parts. This guide walks you through the first full run.

What do I need to get started?

Just an account. Go to app.workforceai.es, choose “Create account” and enter your name, your company name, your email and a password (at least 10 characters). You land in your own office, separate from everyone else’s. For now the AI runs in simulation mode: responses are scripted and realistic, there is no real model behind it, it costs nothing and it never asks for a key.

Being a simulation does not change how you use it. The flow is the same you would have with a real model: you ask, agents collaborate, you approve, and you get a report. That makes it a good way to learn before connecting anything.

Tip. Your office starts with 1,000 welcome coins, so you can try the Shop right away.

How do I ask my office for something?

When the app opens you land on the Office view: a 3D scene with your team. At the bottom there is a text bar to talk to the whole office, the way you would talk to a team lead.

  1. Above the bar you will see example buttons such as "$50,000 proposal", "Sales are down", "Hire 2 people", "Finance" and "Say hi". Press one to try it with a single click.
  2. If you prefer to type, use the bar that says "Write to the office: say hi, ask or request something…" and press Send.
  3. You can say hi, ask a question or request a task. Whoever is relevant answers; if it is a task, the office explains who gets it and why.
Tip. For your first try, start with "$50,000 proposal" and watch who takes each task.

How do I watch the employees collaborate?

Each person in the scene is an agent and moves according to what they are doing: typing at their desk, meeting others to plan, or waiting for your approval.

  1. Look around the office: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom and right-click to pan.
  2. Check the "Live activity" box at the top left. Tasks that start and what the agents do show up there.
  3. Next to the request bar you will see "In progress" with the current state: planning, running or waiting for your approval.
  4. Click an employee to open their panel, see their work and talk to them directly.

What do I do when something needs my approval?

When an agent wants to do something sensitive, it stops and waits at the approvals desk. Nothing continues without your decision.

  1. Look for the pending approvals notice at the bottom left of the office. The "Approvals" counter in the top bar also shows it.
  2. Read the details of what the agent wants to do.
  3. Press Approve to let it continue or Reject to stop it.
  4. If you prefer, decide from the Dashboard, in the Approvals section.
Tip. Rejecting is a valid answer: that is exactly why the human step exists.

Where do I read the report and how do I earn coins?

When a request completes, a report is generated. To read it, switch to Dashboard with the Office / Dashboard toggle in the top bar and open the Reports section.

Finished work also earns you coins. You will see them in the "Coins" counter in the top bar. They are not money and cannot be bought: they are only earned by finished work, and they are used in the office shop.

  1. Wait for the request to reach Completed.
  2. Press Dashboard in the top bar.
  3. Open Reports and pick the report for your request to read it.
  4. Look at the coin counter to see what you earned.

What comes next?

Try your own phrases and watch how the task split changes. If you get lost, the "?" button in the top bar opens a guided tour of each screen.

Keep the real state of the product in mind: it is in early access and the AI runs in simulation until a model provider is configured. What you see is what is already built, with no extra promises.
