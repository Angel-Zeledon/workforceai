AI Workforce OS user guide

Everything you need to get the most out of your office of AI employees, screen by screen. Start with “Getting started” and follow the order, or jump to what interests you.

Start here The 3D office and the Dashboard Approvals and security

Get started

Your first session and how to read the office.

  • Getting started: your first 5 minutes Create your account, ask for your first task, decide an approval, read the report and earn your first coins in about 5 minutes.
  • The 3D office and the Dashboard Move the camera, talk to an employee, follow the activity, adjust the light, redecorate the office and read the Dashboard indicators.

Everyday work

Ask for work, follow it and decide what runs.

  • Projects: big work, tracked Create a project, review its plan and estimated cost, launch it with a budget and follow your agents' progress from a single screen.
  • Chats and Files: pick up conversations and find what was produced Find conversations with your agents and your team, and upload, search, recover and version your office's files.
  • Approvals and security: you stay in control Decide approvals, understand each employee's autonomy, set spend limits, restrict permissions and stop all activity at once if you need to.

Customize

Skills, the shop and your account settings.

  • Skills: reusable know-how for your agents Browse the built-in skills, create or import your own, approve them and assign them to an agent so it works with your method.
  • Coins and the Shop: decorate your office with what your team earns Learn how coins are earned by finished work and how to spend them in the Shop to dress your agents and decorate your office.
  • Settings: configure your office in one place Customize the office, hire employees, choose the AI model, connect tools, protect your account and invite your team from Settings.
