AI Workforce OS user guide

Everything you need to get the most out of your office of AI employees, screen by screen. Start with “Getting started” and follow the order, or jump to what interests you.

  1. 1Getting started: your first 5 minutesCreate your account, ask your office for something, approve what is sensitive, read the report and earn coins. About 5 minutes, nothing to set up.
  2. 2The 3D office and the DashboardMove the camera, open an employee's panel, follow the activity, change the lighting and switch between the office and the Dashboard with its indicators.
  3. 3Projects: big work, with follow-upLearn to create a project, review its plan, launch it with a budget and follow your agents' progress from a single screen.
  4. 4Skills: reusable know-how for your agentsBrowse the Skills library, create or import your own, approve them and assign them to an agent so it works the way you want.
  5. 5Coins and the Shop: decorate your office with what your team earnsLearn how coins are earned by finished work and how to spend them in the Shop to dress your agents and decorate your office.
  6. 6Chats and Files: pick up conversations and find what was producedFind conversations with your agents, write from Chats, and browse, upload, search and recover files with versions and a trash.
  7. 7Approvals and security: you stay in controlLearn to approve sensitive actions, set spend limits, control permissions per employee and stop everything at once if you need to.
  8. 8Settings: configure your office in one placeTour the Settings hub: customize the office, hire employees, use team templates, connect tools and invite your team.
