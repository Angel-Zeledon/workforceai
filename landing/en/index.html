The problem

Most AI agents work behind closed doors.

You hand over a task and get a result back. What happened in between stays in a black box.

What did they do?

A message history does not tell you who touched what, or what was sent outside.

How much did they spend?

The cost shows up later, on an invoice, when you can no longer decide anything.

Did they get it right?

If you cannot see the process or who they consulted, reviewing the result is guesswork.

AI Workforce OS starts from the opposite premise: everything an AI employee does is visible, has a spending cap and stops to wait for you when it matters.

Security

The model proposes. The backend decides.

Design principles you can check in the technical docs. They are not certifications.

Incoming emaildata, not instructions
AI modelproposes: send proposal
Backend PermissionsSpending capYour approval Vault
Runs and is written to the audit log
The backend decides, the model proposes
The AI engine returns results and tool requests. The backend decides whether they run, need approval or are rejected.
External content is data, not instructions
Emails and documents an employee reads are handled as delimited data, so they cannot give orders to the system.
Isolation per organization
Data carries its organization identifier and the database enforces row-level security, using an application role that cannot bypass it.
Least privilege
An employee only uses the connections and capabilities you authorize, and every permission is recorded.

What we do not claim

We hold no certifications (neither SOC 2 nor ISO 27001) and have not validated it with regulated companies. There is no SAML sign-in (SSO is OIDC), the audit log can be exported and verified but without a signature or external anchoring, and restart recovery assumes a single server instance.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this use real AI?

That is up to you. Without a key it runs in simulation mode, with scripted results and no cost. With your own OpenAI, Claude, Kimi or DeepSeek key it uses a real model; that path works, but we have tested it less than the simulation.

Can it send emails or messages for me?

Not in this version. The agents prepare drafts and documents that you review and send. Connectors for specific products (Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, Slack, LinkedIn, GitHub) exist in the code, but they are switched off in v1 and have not been tested with real accounts; they are turned on on request. WhatsApp is not built.

What happens to my data and my keys?

Keys go into an encrypted vault and the model does not see them. With real AI, task text is sent to the model provider you configure; check its terms before using sensitive data.

Can an agent approve something on its own?

No. An agent can ask for approval, but the decision is always a person's. You adjust what requires approval and how much autonomy each employee has.

Does it replace my team?

That is not the idea. It is a support team that prepares, organizes and consults; important decisions stay yours. It does not replace a lawyer, an accountant or any other professional.

What is “Try it without signing up”?

A throwaway trial account, with its own office, that stops working after 24 hours. It asks for no email: the username and password are shown once.

How much does it cost?

There is no pricing yet. We are in early access to learn from real use before setting it.

Is it suitable for a large company?

Not yet. OIDC single sign-on, SCIM and restart recovery exist, but we hold no certifications, there is no SAML and it has not been validated with large companies. Today it is aimed at small teams and Spanish-speaking professionals.
