AI agents usually work behind closed doors.

You hand over a task and get a result. What happened in between is a black box.

What did they do?

A message history does not tell you who touched what, or what was sent outside.

How much did they spend?

The cost shows up later, on an invoice, when you can no longer decide anything.

Did they do it right?

Without seeing the process or who they consulted, reviewing the result is guesswork.

AI Workforce OS starts from the opposite premise: everything an AI employee does is visible, has a spending cap and waits for your decision when it matters.

Three steps, and you stay in charge at each one.

    You ask

What is already built, and why it matters

These capabilities exist in the app's code today. Anything that depends on real integrations is detailed under “Current status”.

A 3D office showing the work live
Every movement comes from real events: who is working, who is consulting whom, who is waiting. If you prefer lists, there is a dashboard mode.
Human approvals on sensitive actions
Sending a proposal or a contract requires your decision. Agents can ask for approval; they can never give it. You choose each employee's autonomy: suggest, approve each action, rules or autonomous.
Estimated cost before running, and caps
You see an estimate before a request runs. There are caps per request, per employee and per organization; when a cap is reached the work pauses and you decide whether it continues.
Credentials kept away from the model
Keys live in an encrypted vault (AES-256-GCM). The AI model never sees them: the backend runs the tools, not the model.
Kill-switch and read-only mode
Freeze activity or put the whole office in read-only mode. You can also pause a single employee or switch off a specific tool.
Audit trail
Every relevant action is recorded in a log the application can only write to, not edit or delete.
Whole projects, with parallel work
A project is split into tasks with dependencies. Whatever does not depend on something else runs at the same time. See the example below.
Workflow templates and packs by business type
Five templates (proposal, new client, collections, month-end close and daily briefing) and four packs (agency, commerce, professional services and general) that set rules and autonomy levels.
Native Spanish, with regional tones
Interface and employees designed for Spanish, with a configurable regional tone. Includes sample tax templates for Mexico and Spain, labeled as examples.

One project, several employees at once

Example: the quarterly financial close, as in the Projects screenshot. The Accountant does not finish one thing before starting the other.

Security and trust

Design principles you can check in the architecture. They are not certifications.

The backend decides, the model proposes
The AI engine returns results and tool requests. The backend decides whether they run, need approval or are rejected.
External content is data, not instructions
Emails and documents an employee reads are handled as delimited data, so they cannot give orders to the system.
Isolation per organization
Data carries its organization identifier and the database enforces row-level security, using an application role that cannot bypass it.
Least privilege
An employee only uses the connections and capabilities you authorize, and every permission is recorded.

What we do not claim

We hold no certifications (neither SOC 2 nor ISO) and we are not ready for regulated enterprise environments. Today there is no single sign-on (SSO), no audit export, and runs in progress do not survive a server restart.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use real AI today?

Not by default: without an API key it runs in simulation mode, with scripted results. Once you set the key, it uses a real model. If you try the demo, you know what to expect.

Can it send emails or messages for me?

The flow exists for Gmail and always asks for your approval, but it has not been tested with real accounts. Do not use it with important email yet. WhatsApp and other channels are planned, not built.

What happens to my data and my keys?

Keys go into an encrypted vault and the model does not see them. With real AI, task text is sent to the model provider you configure; check its terms before using sensitive data.

Can an agent approve something on its own?

No. An agent can ask for approval, but the decision is always a person's. You adjust what requires approval and how much autonomy each employee has.

Does it replace my team?

That is not the idea. It is a support team that prepares, organizes and consults; important decisions stay yours. It does not replace a lawyer, accountant or any other professional.

How much does it cost?

There is no pricing yet. We are opening early access to learn from real use before setting it.

Is it suitable for a large company?

Not today. It is aimed at small teams and Spanish-speaking professionals. SSO, exportable audit and resilience to restarts are among the things still missing.
